# Gerdith's Investigation

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Gerdith loudly orders Molina to accompany her.
- Molina complains and cracks jokes.
- Both armored ghouls move to investigate intelligently and ravenously.

### Trigger

Gerdith is alerted by noise, a winch, a dropped sarcophagus lid, combat, or visible light.
