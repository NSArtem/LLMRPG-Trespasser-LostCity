# Starting the Game

## Operational fields

### Steps

- State that the room is pitch black.
- Apply dehydration to everyone: -4 to all rolls until they drink water; without water they die in 24 hours.
- Begin everyone naked and possessing nothing, except one person has a knife.
- Without a sack or backpack, limit each person to carrying two items.
- Give each player control of 1 free character; their other characters remain restrained.
- Have the Lamb lumber into the room.
- Resolve hiding, noisy flight, or quiet departure as described by the opening situation.

### Trigger

Begin play in 1 BOWLS.
