# Disrupt the Ghost Fence

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Move at least one severed head out of its specific formation.
- While the formation is broken, the fence's magical effects end.
- Cross or move the heads as needed; the source suggests bowling with isopods as an easy way past.
- If the heads are returned to their specific formation, the effects resume, even if the formation is rebuilt elsewhere.

### Trigger

The party needs to pass or neutralize the severed-head formation.
