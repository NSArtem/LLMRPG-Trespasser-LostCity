# Open the Wall at 44A

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Investigate the wall to reveal a loose brick.
- Remove the loose brick; fresh air can then be smelled through the opening.
- With a couple of suitable tools, work for 6 minutes to make an opening large enough for a man to squeeze through.
- Work for 10 minutes instead to make an opening wide enough to fit a table through.
- If the wall is broken down noisily, resolve the delayed hostile arrivals.

### Trigger

The party investigates the newer western brick wall and decides to make a passage through it.
