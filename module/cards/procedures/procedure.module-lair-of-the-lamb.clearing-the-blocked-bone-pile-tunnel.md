# Clearing the Blocked Bone-Pile Tunnel

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Clear the blockage by hand for 10 minutes.
- Reopen the crawl tunnel.

### Trigger

The Lamb has blocked the crawl tunnel between 10 BONE PILE and 14 SARCOPHAGUS with bones.
