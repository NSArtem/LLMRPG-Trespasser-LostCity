# Crush the Lamb with the Noria

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Allow the Lamb to be dragged along with the waterwheel.
- The waterwheel crushes the Lamb against the slotted ceiling.
- If the Lamb is ejected into the White Temple's drinking fountain, expect a swift and forceful response from the Temple.

### Trigger

The party manages to ensnare the Lamb in the noria.
