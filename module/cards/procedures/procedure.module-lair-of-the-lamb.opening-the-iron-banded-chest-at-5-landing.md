# Opening the Iron-Banded Chest at 5 LANDING

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Use a sufficiently destructive method, such as dropping a sarcophagus lid on it, throwing it down 8 PIT, or getting the Lamb to trample it.
- Open the broken chest.
- The force used breaks the vial of lamp oil inside; the fresh torch remains.

### Trigger

The party attempts to open the locked iron-banded chest without an obvious key or normal method.
