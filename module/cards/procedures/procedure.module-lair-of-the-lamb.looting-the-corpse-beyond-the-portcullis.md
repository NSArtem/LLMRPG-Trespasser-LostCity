# Looting the Corpse Beyond the Portcullis

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Pull the corpse to the sealed portcullis.
- Remove items that fit through the bars: sword, bow and arrows, signet ring, and Orb of Indulgence.
- Leave the masterwork breastplate and helmet behind because they are too large to pass through the portcullis.

### Trigger

The party reaches 18A LEDGE and tries to recover the armored corpse’s equipment.
