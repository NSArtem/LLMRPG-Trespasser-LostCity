# Reassemble and Return the Seven Mummies

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Collect the distributed arms, legs, torsos, and heads belonging to seven mummies.
- Reassemble all seven mummies, or come close enough to a full reconstruction.
- Return the reassembled mummies.
- Identify the character who took the most initiative; that character becomes Favored by Vandoh.

### Trigger

The party gathers the scattered mummy parts and missing heads from the crypts and 29 STAGE.
