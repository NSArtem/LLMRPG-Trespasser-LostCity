# Collapsing 12 ABACUS MURAL

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Grab the pike and run.
- Make a Movement check to avoid dying in the collapse.
- Make the room impassable after the ceiling falls.
- If the Lamb is under the collapse, deal 3d6 damage; it digs out in 30 minutes, probably on the north side, and is helpless for 1 round as it exits.

### Trigger

A character removes the 10-foot wooden pike supporting the sagging roof.
