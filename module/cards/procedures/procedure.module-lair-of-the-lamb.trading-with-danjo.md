# Trading with Danjo

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Resolve Danjo’s threat to inform the temple guards by giving him a reason not to.
- Afterward, buy available mundane goods—candles, chickens, bread, and a knife—at the highest price he can demand, never below 20 times market value.
- For other goods, ask Danjo to run across town; delivery takes 1-3 hours depending on the item.
- Conduct the conversation in real time: 1 in-game minute equals 1 minute at the table.

### Trigger

The party opens a conversation with Danjo through 15 CRACK.
