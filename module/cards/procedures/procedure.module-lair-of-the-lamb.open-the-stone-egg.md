# Open the Stone Egg

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Touching any animal carving makes it light up.
- Touch the five egg-laying animals—chicken, snake, octopus, pyorn, and toad—simultaneously.
- On the correct simultaneous input, the egg unfolds like a flower.
- Each set of five incorrect animal touches makes the glow of a touched animal dimmer.
- After four failed attempts, the egg becomes inert for 24 hours before it can be tried again.

### Trigger

Someone interacts with the Stone Egg's animal carvings.
