# Calculate Makeshift Table-Boat Speed

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Start with a speed of 10 feet per round.
- Add 1 foot per round for each paddle, such as a plank, up to a maximum speed of 15 feet per round.
- For each person aboard beyond the second, reduce the speed by 1 foot per round.

### Trigger

The party paddles a table or similar makeshift boat through the cistern.
