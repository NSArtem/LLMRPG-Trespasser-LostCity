# Reach the Elevated Stairs at 46 DAYLIGHT

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Choose either a grappling-hook attempt or a climb up the slick walls.
- For a grappling hook, make an Attack roll against AC 15; success reaches the stairs.
- For a climb, make two Movement checks, one for each 10 feet of the ascent.
- After reaching the stairs, ascend 60 feet to the wooden trapdoor.

### Trigger

A character tries to reach the stone stairs 20 feet above the rubble.
