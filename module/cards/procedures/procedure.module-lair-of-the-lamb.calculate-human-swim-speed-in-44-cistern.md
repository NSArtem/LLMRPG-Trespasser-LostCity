# Calculate Human Swim Speed in 44 CISTERN

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Start with a swim speed of 10 feet per round.
- Reduce the speed by 1 foot per round for every item carried.
- Do not reduce the final swim speed below 5 feet per round.

### Trigger

A human swims through the cistern while carrying items.
