# Disarm the Trap Doors in 24 CRUSH HALLWAY

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Reach 3' into a wall hole to find its switch.
- Continually depress the switch to disarm the corresponding trap door in 24 CRUSH HALLWAY.
- Repeat with the other two holes to hold all three trap doors disabled.

### Trigger

A character uses the three wall holes in 22 MUSHROOM.
