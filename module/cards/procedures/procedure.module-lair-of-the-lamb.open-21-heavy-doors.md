# Open 21 HEAVY DOORS

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Two people can pry the 10' bar aside, opening the doors just enough for a human to squeeze through; the Lamb cannot fit.
- The chains cannot be removed without heavy tools.
- Removing the chains allows the doors to open fully.

### Trigger

The party attempts to pass the barred and chained heavy iron doors.
