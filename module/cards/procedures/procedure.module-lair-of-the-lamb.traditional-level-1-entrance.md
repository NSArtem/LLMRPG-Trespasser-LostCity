# Traditional Level 1 Entrance

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Have the characters enter through the iron door in 5 LANDING.
- The townsfolk lock the door to keep the beast from escaping.
- The townsfolk return to open the door in exactly 12 hours; the characters remain locked in until then.

### Trigger

When running a more traditional game with equipped Level 1 characters.
