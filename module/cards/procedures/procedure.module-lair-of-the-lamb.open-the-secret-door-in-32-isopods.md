# Open the Secret Door in 32 ISOPODS

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Reach to the back of the hole.
- Operate the switch there to open the secret door marked by the worn floor near the east wall.

### Trigger

A character investigates the 5'-deep round hole in the north wall.
