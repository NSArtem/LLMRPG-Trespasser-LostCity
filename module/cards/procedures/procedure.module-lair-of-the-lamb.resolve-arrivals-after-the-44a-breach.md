# Resolve Arrivals After the 44A Breach

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Identify every available ghoul, the apparatus, and the spider crab that is still alive and able to reach 44A.
- Each applicable party arrives in 5+1d4 minutes.
- If multiple parties arrive at the same time, expect possible violence atop the stairs.
- If the Lamb is alive, it also arrives in 5+1d4 minutes, then waits on the bottom of 44 CISTERN near the mouth of the underwater tunnel.

### Trigger

The wall at 44A is broken down noisily.
