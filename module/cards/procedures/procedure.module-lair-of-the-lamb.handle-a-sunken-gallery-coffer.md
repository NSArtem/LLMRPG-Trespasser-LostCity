# Handle a Sunken Gallery Coffer

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Opening a poison-filled coffer forces anyone in front of it to make a Dex save or become poisoned for 1d6 damage per round until a Con check succeeds.
- Opening with a hooked pole or from the side grants a +4 bonus to the Dex check.
- After opening, the poison gas expands to fill the room.
- Characters who flee immediately are not poisoned by the expanding gas.
- The poison gas settles after 10 minutes.

### Trigger

Someone opens one of the statues' stone coffers.
