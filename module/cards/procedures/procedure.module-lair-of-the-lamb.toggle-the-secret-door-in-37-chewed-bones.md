# Toggle the Secret Door in 37 CHEWED BONES

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Reach 4' into each of the two holes to find a switch.
- Press both switches simultaneously to open the 2' by 2' panel 8' above the floor.
- Press both switches simultaneously again to close the door.

### Trigger

The party removes the two broken chair legs from the north-wall holes.
