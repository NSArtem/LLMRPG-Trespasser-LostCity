# Move a Crypt Sarcophagus Lid

## Operational fields

### Steps

- One person can move a lid with a successful Str check.
- Six people can gently set the lid on the floor or pick it up.

### Trigger

The party attempts to open or move a stone sarcophagus lid in 25 CRYPT.
