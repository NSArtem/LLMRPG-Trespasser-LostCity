# Operate the Shroud Guillotine

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Turn the winch; it sounds and feels as though a blade is being raised.
- Continue for about 12 seconds.
- The unseen 'blade' falls with a heavy thunk.
- If a person's head is in the guillotine at that moment, apply the soul-severance effect; the head and neck remain intact.

### Trigger

Someone turns the winch on the blade-less guillotine.
