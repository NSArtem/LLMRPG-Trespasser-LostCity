# Searching 10 BONE PILE

## Operational fields

### Steps

- For every man-hour spent searching, roll 1d20.
- Consult the Bone Pile Search table for the result.
- Any amount of searching also reveals the small crawl tunnel to 14 SARCOPHAGUS.

### Trigger

A character spends time searching the bone-pellet pile.
