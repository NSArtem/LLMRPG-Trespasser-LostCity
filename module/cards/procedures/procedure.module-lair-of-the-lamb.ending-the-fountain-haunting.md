# Ending the Fountain Haunting

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Locate the armored skeleton in the shallows by investigating the pool.
- Either remove it from the water and pray over it sincerely, or destroy it.
- If it is removed and prayed over, even the shortest sincere prayer ends the haunting.
- If it is destroyed, the haunting ends, then the destroyer makes a Charisma Save; on a failure, the destroyer becomes permanently afraid of water.

### Trigger

The party attempts to end the haunting in 9 FOUNTAIN.
