# Using the Sarcophagus Trapdoor

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Replace the bladed lid.
- The bottom swings open and drops the occupant 10 feet onto the rugs in 14A SHADRAKUL; ropes crossing the lid are severed.
- Remove the lid again to swing the bottom shut.
- If the black iron spellbook is held, the trapdoor locks open.

### Trigger

A person enters the sarcophagus in 14 SARCOPHAGUS.
