# Seek entry when not on the guest list

- ID: `winters-daughter.procedure.area-16-gain-entry-with-mushroom`
- Type: `procedure`
- Source pages: 31
- References: `winters-daughter.actor.area-16-griddlegrim`, `winters-daughter.location.area-16-entrance-hall`

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Accept a mushroom from the goblin doorman’s pouch.
- Eat the mushroom.
- The visitor may then be allowed entry.

### Trigger

A visitor who is not on the guest list seeks entry through area 16.
