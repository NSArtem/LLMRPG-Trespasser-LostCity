## Operational fields

### Description

A decaying but once-solid set of wooden pews faces a stone altar. The altar bears a statue of St Sedge, a holy crusader holding a thick red candle aloft; dried red wax has run over the statue and altar, and the velvet cloth is disintegrating. A ragged tapestry depicts the gates of heaven, where Sir Chyde and St Sedge meet in death. A locked wooden door, swollen with damp, is concealed behind the tapestry.
