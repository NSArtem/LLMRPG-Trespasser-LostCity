## Operational fields

### Text

The stone coffer is carved with Sir Chyde's likeness and leaf patterns and contains his skeleton. On its wrists are two copper bracelets engraved with owls whose amethyst eyes make each bracelet worth 1,000gp. On one finger is a bronze band set with a moonstone and branch-like fittings: the Ring of Soul-Binding. The restored portrait is worth 1,500gp. Each cleaned silver candlestick is worth 200gp.
