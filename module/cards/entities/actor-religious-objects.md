## Operational fields

### Role

The four mould-covered religious objects animate as flying attackers and shrill, sanctimonious scolds, condemning the characters' misdeeds, including entering the tomb. Statistics: AC 5 [14], HD 1 (hp 3, 3, 4, 5), Att 1 × bash (1d4), THAC0 19 [0], MV 90’ (30’) flying, SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (1), ML 12, AL Lawful, XP 10. When one is killed, it erupts in mould spores; anyone in melee must save versus poison or suffer 1d2 damage and choke for 1 round, during which they cannot act.
