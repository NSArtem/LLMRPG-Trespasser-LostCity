## Operational fields

### Role

Proud frost elf warriors who serve as guests and guards. A random event may bring 1d3 knights from the forest on white chargers; at the feast, 5 knights sit as guests and 4 guards stand at the edges, ready to act. Combat profile: AC 3 [16], HD 2, attack 1 × spear or lance (1d6), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 60’ (20’), saves D12 W13 P13 B15 S15 (E2), ML 9, Neutral, XP 20.
