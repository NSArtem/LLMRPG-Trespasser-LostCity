## Operational fields

### Trigger

Sir Chyde is slain in the final battle shortly after Snowfall-at-Dusk is imprisoned. He asks to be interred in a tomb by the Whything Stones with the portrait and ring, with a tomb section for his betrothed and a chapel of St Sedge; the tomb later serves as a pilgrimage site with a resident priest.
