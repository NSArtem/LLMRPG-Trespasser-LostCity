# Disturbed mould releases choking spores

- ID: `situation-area-11-mould-spores`
- Type: `situation`
- Source pages: 18
- References: `location-area-11-statues-with-weapons`, `procedure-clear-area-11-mould`

## Operational fields

### Trigger

Disturbing the yellow-and-purple wall mould raises a spore cloud; everyone within 10 feet must save versus poison or suffer 1d4 damage and violent coughing and choking for 1 turn.
