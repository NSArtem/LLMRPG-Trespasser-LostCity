# The slime vapour removes weight

- ID: `situation-family-crypt-slime-contact`
- Type: `situation`
- Source pages: 16
- References: `actor-floating-skeletons`, `location-area-08-family-crypt`

## Operational fields

### Trigger

An object touching the slime becomes weightless for a moment. If bare flesh touches it, the slime instantly coats the character's whole body; the character becomes permanently lighter than air and drifts to the ceiling until the slime is removed, for example by dispel magic.
