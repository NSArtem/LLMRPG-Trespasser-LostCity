### Description

A lavish feast room dominated by a table laden with roast swans stuffed with blackbirds, syrup-dripping fruit, ice wines in buckets, and violet spirits in crystal decanters. Five frost elf knights and seven frost elf nobles wait in subdued silence; the princess’s guests have already waited for centuries.
