# Recover the Ring for the Wizard

- ID: `procedure-tomb-robbers-mission`
- Type: `procedure`
- Source pages: 8
- References: `actor-wizard-patron`, `item-ring-of-soul-binding`, `knowledge-ring-theft-complication`, `location-sir-chyde-tomb`

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Use the wizard's map to reach the old burial mound in Dolmenwood.
- Enter the tomb.
- Take the bronze, moonstone-set ring with woven-branch fittings from the finger of the buried skeleton.
- Return the ring to the wizard for 5,000 gp, or another reward amount chosen by the referee.

### Trigger

The PCs accept the wizard's employment.
