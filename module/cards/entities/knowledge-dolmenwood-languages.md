## Operational fields

### Text

Woldish is the common tongue. Old Woldish is an ancient dialect largely incomprehensible to Woldish speakers. Drunic is the Drune's secret language. Liturgic is sacred to the Church of the One True God. Sylvan is common among fairies and fairy-kin, though mortal speakers invariably sound foolish. High Elfish is the language of fairy nobility and is utterly unfathomable to mortals. The Immortal Tongue of Fairy is an ancient, reality-bending language known only to the oldest and most dreadful fairies.
