# The Ring Stolen

- ID: `situation.epilogue.ring-stolen`
- Type: `situation`
- Source pages: 25
- References: `effect.ring.ghost-haunting`, `item.ring-of-soul-binding`, `procedure.epilogue.end-haunting`

## Operational fields

### Trigger

Someone removes the Ring of Soul-Binding from the tomb and keeps it. Sir Chyde’s ghost visits the possessor nightly, first pleading for the ring’s return and promising his fairy bride’s generosity, then becoming angry and violent if refused.
