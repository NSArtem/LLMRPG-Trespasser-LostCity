### Text

The mural depicts Sir Chyde mounted in battle against a fairy army, accompanied by two hunting hounds. When cleared, his raised hand is shown holding the same wavy-grooved longsword as the statue weapon, glowing white, and one hound's collar gives the name Chedr.
