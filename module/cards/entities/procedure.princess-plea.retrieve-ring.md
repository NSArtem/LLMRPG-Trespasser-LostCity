# Fulfil the Princess’s Plea

- ID: `procedure.princess-plea.retrieve-ring`
- Type: `procedure`
- Source pages: 24
- References: `actor.princess-snowfall-at-dusk`, `item.ring-of-soul-binding`, `situation.epilogue.lovers-reunited`

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Return through the warded route to Sir Chyde’s tomb.
- Take the ring from Sir Chyde’s coffer.
- Bring the ring into Fairy; Sir Chyde’s soul follows it, allowing the lovers to reunite and marry.
- The princess may reward the characters with jewels or by granting one wish.

### Trigger

Princess Snowfall-at-Dusk asks the characters to reunite her with Sir Chyde.
