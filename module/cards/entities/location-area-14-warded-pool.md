### Description

From the mortal side, area 14 appears as a vaulted chamber filled by a shimmering pool, with a white-marble statue of a long-haired, robed maiden bearing a star on her brow at its centre. At the foot of the stairs, dozens of ghostly candles float in mid-air and form an unavoidable ward.
