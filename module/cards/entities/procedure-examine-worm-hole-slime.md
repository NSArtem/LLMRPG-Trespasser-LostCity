# Examine the worm-hole slime trails

- ID: `procedure-examine-worm-hole-slime`
- Type: `procedure`
- Source pages: 13
- References: `actor-wormtongues`, `location-area-04-worm-hole`

## Operational fields

### Steps

- A character skilled in tracking can identify trails of three slug- or worm-like creatures leading from the hole into the woods and back.
- Bare-flesh contact reveals slight acidity, but the slime is not acidic enough to inflict damage.

### Trigger

Characters track or touch the slime trails by area 4.
