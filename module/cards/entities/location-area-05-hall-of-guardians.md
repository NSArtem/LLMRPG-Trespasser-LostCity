## Operational fields

### Description

A musty, wet chamber with dank, stifling air and wet walls and ceiling. Four approximately 1-foot-tall religious objects stand on corner plinths under mustard-coloured mould: a silver crucifix worth 200gp, a wooden cherub statue, a holy book, and a huge candle. Heavy damp-streaked stone double doors bear the inscription “The Most Dear”. The dusty floor conceals a mosaic and old drag scratches.
