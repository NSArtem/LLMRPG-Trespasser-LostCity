### Text

The writing desk's locked drawer is easily smashed open. Inside is an old mould-covered book whose damp-stuck pages are indecipherable, but a brass sheet inside the cover bears a pastoral poem about Sir Chyde hunting with his favoured dogs. One dog is named Flaegr.
