### Description

An ancient stone circle in a Dolmenwood clearing where Sir Chyde and Snowfall-at-Dusk first met. Its mossy stones bear moonlight-reflecting eldritch runes and hidden carvings of tormented icy fey; the Drune now frequent and guard the site.
