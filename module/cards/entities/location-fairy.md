## Operational fields

### Description

The immortal realm adjoining the mortal world near Dolmenwood, also sometimes called Elfland. It is endless and timeless, inhabited by elves and many other fairy beings, generally intangible to mortals except at special places or times where the realms overlap and permit transit.
