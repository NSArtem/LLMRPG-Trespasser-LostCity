# Pass Through the Ghostly Candle Ward

- ID: `procedure.area15.pass-candle-ward`
- Type: `procedure`
- Source pages: 21
- References: `location.area15.warded-paths`

## Operational fields

### Steps

- A character who came from the tomb can pass through the ward; it is impassable to everyone else.
- A permitted traveller appears at the bottom of the stairs in area 14.
- On arrival, 1d6 days have passed in the mortal world.

### Trigger

A character attempts to enter one of the forest paths through the floating candles.
