### Role

Two 6-foot-tall stone constructs guard the doors in area 12. Statistics: AC 3 [16], HD 4* (hp 17, 19), Att 1 × bite (1d8), THAC0 16 [+3], MV 120’ (40’), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (4), ML 12, AL Neutral, XP 125. Their chains prevent them from leaving the room. As constructs, they are harmed only by magic and are unaffected by charms and mind control.
