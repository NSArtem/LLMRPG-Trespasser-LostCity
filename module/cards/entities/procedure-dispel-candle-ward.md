# Dispel the candle ward

- ID: `procedure-dispel-candle-ward`
- Type: `procedure`
- Source pages: 20
- References: `location-area-14-warded-pool`

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Fairy magic cannot dispel the ward.
- Other magic can dispel it; treat the ward as if cast by a 10th-level cleric.

### Trigger

Characters attempt to dispel the ward at the bottom of the stairs.
