## Operational fields

### Description

A white-marble statue depicts a fair maiden with long flowing hair and robe, a star on her brow, and one finger raised to her lips in a plea for silence. A black cloth blindfold covers its eyes. The statue stands on a 3-foot-wide, 1-foot-high round marble plinth. Pristine stairs descend toward area 14 beneath an arch carved as interwoven tree branches; illumination reveals reflections shimmering from water below.
