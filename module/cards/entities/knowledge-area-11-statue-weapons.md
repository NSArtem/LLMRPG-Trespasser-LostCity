# Weapons held by the seven footmen

- ID: `knowledge-area-11-statue-weapons`
- Type: `knowledge`
- Source pages: 18
- References: `knowledge-area-11-battle-mural`, `location-area-11-statues-with-weapons`

## Operational fields

### Text

The statues hold: a flanged mace with a spiralling hilt; a morningstar with 2-inch spikes; a battle axe engraved with a horse's head; a warhammer whose head is shaped like a boar; a longsword with wavy blood grooves; a halberd with a mouldy pennant; and a spear with a serrated blade. The fairy-made longsword is enchanted, granting +2 to hit and damage. Its glamour affects mortal wielders and compels them to attack the largest foe in battle.
