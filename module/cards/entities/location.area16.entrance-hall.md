### Description

The tower entrance is decorated with garlands of white roses and pale blue forget-me-nots tied with ribbons. Dozens of winter hats and coats crowd wall hooks; boots and ice skates line shoe racks. A fireplace burns melting icicles with electric-blue flames. Baking aromas drift from area 17, while muted voices come from the stairs to area 18.
