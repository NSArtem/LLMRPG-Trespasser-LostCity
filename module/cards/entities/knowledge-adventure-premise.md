### Text

Winter's Daughter is a short Dolmenwood adventure about the forbidden love between the mortal knight Sir Chyde and a fairy princess during the ancient war between mortals and fairies. Sir Chyde's sealed tomb has remained closed for centuries and contains secrets and treasure.
