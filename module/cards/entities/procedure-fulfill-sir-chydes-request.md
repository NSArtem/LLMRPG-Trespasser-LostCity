# Carry Sir Chyde’s ring toward his beloved

- ID: `procedure-fulfill-sir-chydes-request`
- Type: `procedure`
- Source pages: 19
- References: `actor-ghost-of-sir-chyde`, `knowledge-sir-chyde-and-imprisoned-princess`, `location-area-13-knights-tomb`, `location-area-14-warded-pool`, `situation-sir-chydes-ring-removed`

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Take the bronze moonstone ring from the stone coffer.
- Carry it to the lower tomb level at area 14.
- Use the stairs in area 6 or area 7 to enter Fairy, where Sir Chyde believes the imprisoned princess waits.

### Trigger

The ghost asks a living person to help him join the princess in Fairy.
