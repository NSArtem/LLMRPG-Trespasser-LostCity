## Operational fields

### Role

A jovial Dolmenwood goblin doorman with a large head, spindly neck, dark grey warty skin, huge yellow eyes, and purple velvet finery. He rides on Grimmlegridge’s back, checks every visitor’s name, admits invited wedding guests, and offers uninvited visitors entry if they eat a mushroom from his pouch. Combat profile: AC 7 [12], HD 1* (4 hp), attack 1 × spear (1d6) or 1 × spell, THAC0 19 [0], MV 90’ (30’), saves D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (1), ML 7, Neutral, XP 13; spells charm person, darkness, sleep, and phantasmal force.
