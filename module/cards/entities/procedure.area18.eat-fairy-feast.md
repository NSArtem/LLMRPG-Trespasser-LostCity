# Eat or Drink at the Fairy Feast

- ID: `procedure.area18.eat-fairy-feast`
- Type: `procedure`
- Source pages: 23
- References: `location.area18.wedding-feast`

## Operational fields

### Steps

- The mortal makes a save versus spells.
- On a failed save, the mortal forever yearns to return to Fairy.

### Trigger

A mortal consumes food or drink from the wedding feast.
