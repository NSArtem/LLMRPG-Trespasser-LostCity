# The Princess’s Magical Ban

- ID: `knowledge.princess.magical-ban`
- Type: `knowledge`

## Operational fields

### Text

Princess Snowfall-at-Dusk cannot leave the glade containing her tower unless she renounces her love for Sir Chyde. The ban also defeats magical summoning and teleportation, including the Ring of Soul-Binding’s full-moon power to unite bound persons.
