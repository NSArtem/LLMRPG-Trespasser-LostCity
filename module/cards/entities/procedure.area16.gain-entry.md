# Gain Entry to the Wedding

- ID: `procedure.area16.gain-entry`
- Type: `procedure`
- Source pages: 22
- References: `actor.griddlegrim`, `item.pouch-of-limitless-fungi`, `table.pouch.random-shroom-effects`

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Griddlegrim checks the visitor’s name.
- Invited wedding guests are admitted.
- An uninvited person may enter by eating one mushroom from Griddlegrim’s Pouch of Limitless Fungi and resolving its magic.

### Trigger

A visitor asks Griddlegrim to enter the tower.
