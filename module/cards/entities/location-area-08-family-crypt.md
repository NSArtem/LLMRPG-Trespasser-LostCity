## Operational fields

### Description

Two floating skeletons dance above a fissure in a family crypt containing five stone coffers with tarnished brass portrait plaques. Coffers A and B are open with their lids pushed aside. Transparent slime drips from a hairline ceiling crack, coats the skeletons, and falls into a fissure of initially indeterminate depth; glimmering light below resembles sunlight reflected from snow.
