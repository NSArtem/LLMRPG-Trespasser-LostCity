# Wards Between the Tomb and Frigia

- ID: `knowledge.frigia-door.wards`
- Type: `knowledge`
- Source pages: 25
- References: `location.frigia.doorway-from-dolmenwood`, `procedure.area15.dispel-candle-ward`

## Operational fields

### Text

The wards set when Sir Chyde’s tomb was sealed stop natives of Frigia from passing between Fairy and the mortal world, but mortals may travel from Dolmenwood into Frigia. Fairy magic cannot dispel these wards, though mortal magic may be able to do so.
