### Description

A burial mound in the glade beside the Whything Stones. It includes Sir Chyde's burial, a subsection dedicated to his betrothed, and a chapel of St Sedge. Once maintained as a pilgrimage site, it was later warded and sealed after fey influence and hauntings.
