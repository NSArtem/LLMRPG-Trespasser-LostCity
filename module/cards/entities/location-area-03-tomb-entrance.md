## Operational fields

### Description

A granite slab seals the burial mound and is overgrown with lichen and sweet-smelling wild roses. Beyond it, stairs descend 20 feet into the earth through centuries of undisturbed dust, deathly silence, and a moist, mouldy smell. Scratches on the stairs indicate that something heavy was dragged upward long ago.
