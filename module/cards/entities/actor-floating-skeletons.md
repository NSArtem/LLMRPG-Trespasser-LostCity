## Operational fields

### Role

Two moisture-slick skeletons waltz arm in arm in mid-air above the fissure. One wears a pearl necklace and the other a gold medallion, each worth 500gp. They welcome strangers to join the dance, speak old-fashioned Woldish (Common, not Old Woldish) in a distant whisper, and attack vehemently if any coffer is disturbed. Statistics: AC 8 [11], HD 2 (hp 8, 10), Att 1 × claws (1d4), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 90’ (30’) flying, SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (2), ML 12, AL Neutral, XP 20. As undead they are unaffected by charms and mind control. Any weapon that hits one in melee becomes weightless for a moment, imposing –1 to that weapon's attack roll in the next round.
