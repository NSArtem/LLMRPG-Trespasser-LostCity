## Operational fields

### Role

An ageless, benevolent frost elf princess with winter-sun blonde hair, scintillating pale skin, crystalline blue eyes, a flowing white gown, and a crystal star bound to her brow. She is dignified, hopeful despite her imprisonment, and excited by the possibilities presented by mortal strangers. She wants reunion with Sir Chyde. Combat profile: AC 7 [12], HD 5* (19 hp), attack 1 × icicle dagger (1d4) or 1 × spell, THAC0 15 [+4], MV 120’ (40’), saves D10 W11 P11 B13 S12 (E5), ML 8, Neutral, XP 300; spells charm person, sleep, hold person, and invisibility. She can grant one other person’s wish, but may use that power only once ever.
