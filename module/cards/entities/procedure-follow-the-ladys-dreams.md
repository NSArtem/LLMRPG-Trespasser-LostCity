# Follow the Lady's Dream Instructions

- ID: `procedure-follow-the-ladys-dreams`
- Type: `procedure`
- Source pages: 8
- References: `item-ring-of-soul-binding`, `location-door-to-fairy`, `location-sir-chyde-tomb`, `situation-hook-dreams-of-the-lady`

## Operational fields

### Steps

- In a second dream, the PC sees themself enter a burial mound, open a coffer, remove a ring from a skeleton's finger, and bring it to the Lady.
- The Lady says the tomb is real and that a magical door to her realm lies on its lower level.
- She falsely promises her heart if the PC retrieves the ring that she says was stolen from her in ancient times.
- She reveals the tomb's location and says a bearer of the ring can enter the lower-level door into her realm in Fairy.

### Trigger

The chosen PC has experienced the recurring romantic dream for several weeks.
