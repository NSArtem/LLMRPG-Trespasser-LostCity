### Description

A snow-clad glade lies in an ominous fir wood. A white marble tower with 3 floors stands in the middle of a frozen lake beneath a purple fissure in the sky. Ghostly floating votive candles ward the forest paths; passing through them transports eligible characters to the tomb at area 14.
