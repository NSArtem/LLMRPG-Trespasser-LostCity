## Operational fields

### Role

A pale, semi-transparent azure, thin, plate-armoured ghost with raised visor, forlorn and love-lost, kneels before the portrait. He beseeches the characters to help him reach his beloved in Fairy and does not appreciate tomb robbers. Statistics: AC 3 [16], HD 5* (26 hp), Att 1 × touch (1d6 + aging), THAC0 15 [+4], MV 60’ (20’), SV D10 W11 P12 B13 S14 (5), ML 11, AL Lawful, XP 300. His touch ages a mortal target 2d20 years; a fairy does not age but must save versus spells or suffer terror for 1d6 rounds. As incorporeal undead, he is harmed only by magic or silver and is unaffected by charms and mind control. Turning makes him disappear for 24 hours. He is tethered to the ring in the coffer and cannot exist more than 10 feet from it.
