# End Sir Chyde’s Haunting

- ID: `procedure.epilogue.end-haunting`
- Type: `procedure`
- Source pages: 25
- References: `effect.ring.unbinding`, `situation.epilogue.ring-stolen`

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Either slay the ghost, or undo the soul-binding on the ring.
- Unbinding may be achieved through one of the ring’s stated release conditions.

### Trigger

The possessor of the stolen Ring of Soul-Binding is being haunted by Sir Chyde’s ghost.
