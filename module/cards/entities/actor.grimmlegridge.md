## Operational fields

### Role

A hulking, obese, 10-foot-tall Dolmenwood troll with hairless clay-like flesh, filthy ragged hessian clothes, and a bulging pouch of moss-covered humanoid bones. He obeys Griddlegrim but secretly wants to crush the characters and add their bodies to his moss-corpse collection. Combat profile: AC 6 [13], HD 8* (45 hp), attacks 2 × fists (1d10 plus moss growth), THAC0 12 [+7], MV 120’ (40’), saves D8 W9 P10 B10 S12 (8), ML 9, Chaotic, XP 1,200. A touched mortal sprouts moss at the contact point. He regenerates 3 hp per round and reforms unless killed with fire or acid.
