### Description

Pillars carved with scenes of holy war against fairies flank massive, smooth stone double doors. The doors are locked and magically warded, with “Call to the Companions” cut deeply into them. Two larger-than-life, 6-foot-tall stone hounds are chained to the base of the doors.
