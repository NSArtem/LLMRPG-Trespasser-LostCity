# Investigate or enter the worm hole

- ID: `procedure-investigate-worm-hole`
- Type: `procedure`
- Source pages: 13
- References: `actor-wormtongues`, `location-area-04-worm-hole`, `location-area-10-abandoned-priests-quarters`

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Illumination reveals a 15-foot descent to area 10.
- Making noise attracts the wormtongues lurking inside.
- Crawling down disturbs the wormtongues and leads to area 10 after 15 feet.

### Trigger

Characters illuminate the hole, make noise near it, or crawl down it.
