# Raid the Fairy Pantry

- ID: `procedure.area17.raid-pantry`
- Type: `procedure`
- Source pages: 23
- References: `location.area17.fairy-kitchen`

## Operational fields

### Steps

- The pantry appears larger the farther one explores it.
- For each person and each turn spent searching, roll a 1-in-6 chance of finding treasure worth 2d10 × 10 gp in the mortal world.
- Possible finds include rare fairy wines, jellied dreams, sprite dust, and tarts of infatuation.

### Trigger

A character spends time searching deeper into the pantry.
