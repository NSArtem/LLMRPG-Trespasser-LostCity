### Steps

- Clearing the knight’s hand reveals a longsword with wavy blood grooves surrounded by a white glow.
- Clearing the hounds reveals the name Chedr written on one hound’s collar.
- Resolve disturbance of the mould using its poison-spore hazard.

### Trigger

Characters remove mould from Sir Chyde's raised hand or the painted hounds.
