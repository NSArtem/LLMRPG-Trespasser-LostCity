# The Mortal World and Fairy

- ID: `knowledge-two-realms`
- Type: `knowledge`
- Source pages: 10
- References: `location-dolmenwood`, `location-door-to-fairy`, `location-fairy`

## Operational fields

### Text

Dolmenwood occupies a mortal region where Fairy is close enough that its inhabitants may be heard but normally cannot be touched or reached by mortals. Only certain places or times overlap, allowing passage between realms; the adventure contains one such place. Calling Fairy 'Elfland' is imprecise because elves are not its only inhabitants.
