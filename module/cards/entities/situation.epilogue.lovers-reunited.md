### Trigger

The Ring of Soul-Binding is carried from Sir Chyde’s tomb into Fairy. The mortal and fairy regions then begin to separate; within a day or two, the passages between areas 14 and 15 vanish and area 14 again becomes a vaulted chamber with a pool.
