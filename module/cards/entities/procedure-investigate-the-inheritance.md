# Investigate the Inherited Charter

- ID: `procedure-investigate-the-inheritance`
- Type: `procedure`
- Source pages: 8
- References: `actor-brigford-the-wise`, `item-ring-of-soul-binding`, `location-door-to-fairy`, `location-sir-chyde-tomb`, `situation-hook-inheritance`

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Review an old charter and family tree claiming that the PC descends from Brigford the Wise, Sir Chyde's brother.
- Use the charter to locate the tomb where Brigford is interred beside his brother.
- Note the charter's claim that Sir Chyde's fabled sword and ring legally belong to the inheriting PC.
- Note the documents' claim that the ring can open a doorway to Fairy.

### Trigger

The PC receives the deceased relative's papers and oddments.
