## Operational fields

### Role

Three 4-foot-long, thigh-thick, dark-pink, bumpy, eyeless worms with lamprey-like tooth-ringed mouths lurk in the narrow tunnel by area 10 and can be attracted or disturbed from area 4. They attack by taste and ravenously attack when noise disturbs them. Statistics: AC 7 [12], HD 1+1* (hp 3, 4, 5), Att 1 × bite (1d4 + acid), THAC0 18 [+1], MV 120’ (40’), SV D12 W13 P14 B15 S16 (1), ML 7, AL Neutral, XP 19. Their acid causes 1 damage per round until washed off, for example with water.
