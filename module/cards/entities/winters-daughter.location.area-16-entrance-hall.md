## Operational fields

### Description

Griddlegrim, a goblin doorman, rides on the back of the troll Grimmlegridge. The hall smells of cooking from area 17 and is decorated with garlands and rows of cosy boots and coats. Visitors not on the guest list may be admitted if they eat a mushroom from Griddlegrim’s pouch.
