# Bypass or reverse the freezing mirror

- ID: `procedure-counter-freezing-mirror`
- Type: `procedure`
- Source pages: 15
- References: `location-area-07-freezing-mirror`, `situation-freezing-mirror-effect`

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Cover the mirror to bypass its freezing effect.
- Unfreeze an affected person with holy water, cure light wounds, or sunlight.

### Trigger

Characters need to pass the mirror safely or free someone frozen by it.
