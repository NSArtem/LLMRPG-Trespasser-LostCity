# Dispel the Ghostly Candle Ward

- ID: `procedure.area15.dispel-candle-ward`
- Type: `procedure`
- Source pages: 21
- References: `knowledge.frigia-door.wards`, `location.area15.warded-paths`

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Fairy magic cannot affect the ward.
- Other magic may dispel it.
- Resolve dispelling as though the ward were cast by a 10th-level cleric.

### Trigger

Someone attempts to remove the ward blocking the forest paths.
