# Warped reality at the Warded Pool

- ID: `knowledge-warded-pool-warped-reality`
- Type: `knowledge`
- Source pages: 20
- References: `knowledge-area-13-tomb-goods`, `location-area-14-warded-pool`

## Operational fields

### Text

The apparent vaulted pool is the tomb's bottom level as perceived before passing through the ward in the mortal world. Centuries of action by the Ring of Soul-Binding have warped reality so that Fairy is now more present here than the mortal world. The Church placed the stair-bottom wards when sealing the tomb to prevent further encroachment from Fairy.
