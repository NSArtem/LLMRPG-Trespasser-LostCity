# Answer the Hall of Hounds riddle

- ID: `procedure-open-hall-of-hounds-doors`
- Type: `procedure`
- Source pages: 18
- References: `knowledge-area-11-battle-mural`, `knowledge-flaegr-poem`, `location-area-12-hall-of-hounds`

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Speak the names “Flaegr and Chedr”, in either order.
- The doors open when both names are spoken.

### Trigger

Characters attempt to open the locked, magically warded double doors in area 12.
