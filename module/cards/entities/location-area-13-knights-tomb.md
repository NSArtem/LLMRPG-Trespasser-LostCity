### Description

Sir Chyde's ghost kneels before a dust-grimed portrait of a fair blond maiden in a white robe with a star on her brow, standing amid the Whything Stones of area 2. A carved stone coffer holds the knight's skeleton and grave goods. Two 4-foot-tall tarnished silver candlesticks stand on the floor.
