## Operational fields

### Text

The mosaic shows Sir Chyde on a white charger piercing a fairy knight's heart. Its Old Woldish inscription reads, “Here lies the noble Sir Chyde, slayer of Frost, defender of the King”. Speakers of modern Woldish can recognize the name and the words for “Frost” and “King”. Scratches indicate that something heavy was dragged from area 7 a long time ago.
