## Operational fields

### Trigger

Mortal armies win the war and exile the Cold Prince to his dominion in Fairy. He has not openly troubled Dolmenwood since, but common folk fear his return; folklore says the banishing magic weakens each winter and frigid winds whisper of his desire to reclaim the mortal kingdom.
