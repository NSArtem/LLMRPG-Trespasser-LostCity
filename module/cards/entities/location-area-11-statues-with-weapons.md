## Operational fields

### Description

Seven smooth, finely carved dark-stone statues of footmen hold real weapons. The walls bear vivid yellow and purple mould patches. A faded mural shows Sir Chyde on horseback fighting a fairy army with two hunting hounds at his side; mould obscures his raised right hand and parts of the hounds.
