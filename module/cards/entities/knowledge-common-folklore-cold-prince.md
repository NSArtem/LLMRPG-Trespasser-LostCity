# Common Folklore: The Cold Prince

- Source pages: 9

## Operational fields

### Text

Common folk say the Cold Prince ruled Dolmenwood before mortals, covering it in eternal frost and snow. About 900 years ago, mortals fought his fairy armies for control of the forest and ultimately exiled him to Fairy. They fear that the banishment weakens in winter and that he still seeks to reclaim his kingdom.
