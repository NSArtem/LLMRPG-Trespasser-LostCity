# Open the tomb entrance slab

- ID: `procedure-open-tomb-entrance-slab`
- Type: `procedure`
- Source pages: 13
- References: `location-area-03-tomb-entrance`

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Move the slab with a cumulative STR bonus of at least 4.
- Alternatively, break the slab over 6 turns; cumulative STR bonuses reduce this time, to a minimum of 2 turns.

### Trigger

Characters attempt to move or break the granite slab sealing area 3.
