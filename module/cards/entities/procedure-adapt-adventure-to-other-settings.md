# Adapt the Adventure to Another Setting

- ID: `procedure-adapt-adventure-to-other-settings`
- Type: `procedure`
- Source pages: 5
- References: `actor-cold-prince`, `location-whything-stones`

## Operational fields

### Steps

- Place the adventure in a cool region where icy fairies and rings of ancient standing stones are plausible.
- Integrate the background into the chosen setting.
- Replace the Cold Prince with another wicked fairy king when appropriate.

### Trigger

The referee is running the adventure outside Dolmenwood.
