# The Ghouls of the Second Floor

## Operational fields

### Text

Five ghouls are trapped between 21 HEAVY DOOR and 40 GHOST FENCE. Jasper and Luntz wander the halls playing at being gentlemen; Gerdith and Molina guard the ballista; Shawson is feral and imprisoned in 39 STONE EGG; Captain Conroy is absent after ascending to moldhood. The surviving ghouls were soldiers. Hunger can overwhelm them, but after consuming a large amount of meat they become lucid, conversant, compassionate, and remorseful. Fresh meat, respectful treatment, and military-style parlay can be used to befriend any of them except Shawson.
