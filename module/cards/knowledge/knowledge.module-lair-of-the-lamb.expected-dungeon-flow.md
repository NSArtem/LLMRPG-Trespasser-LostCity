# Expected Dungeon Flow

## Operational fields

### Text

The expected but not required sequence is: flee or hide, then return to free captives; lure the Lamb away from 9 FOUNTAIN and drink; follow the main path to 13 CITY MURAL, where the bashed-in door reinforces that locked doors can be broken; and escape through 21 HEAVY DOOR. Equipment such as light, rope, and weapons gates progress. The dungeon has one valuable but imperfect loop through a secret tunnel that the Lamb can eventually block. Deaths are expected, and the Lamb stopping to eat helps prevent a total party kill.
