# Fresh Air and the Cistern Beyond 44A

## Operational fields

### Text

Investigating the newer wall reveals a loose brick. Removing it releases the smell of fresh air, and torchlight through a larger opening reveals a huge water-filled room receding into darkness with two water-stained pillars at the light's edge.
