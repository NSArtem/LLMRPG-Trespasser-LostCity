# Ghoul Favors and Rewards

## Operational fields

### Text

The ghouls first ask for human meat, lots of meat, destruction of the ghost fence in 40 GHOST FENCE, and humiliation of the spider crab in 31 TRAVERSE. If at least 3 are achieved, they also ask the party to beat Gerdith at a miniatures wargame in 28 GAMES, outperform Jasper at a show in 29 STAGE, and destroy the Lamb with cautious ghoul assistance. Each killed ghoul deducts one favor. At 4 favors they try very hard not to eat the party, requiring a Cha save to resist. At 5 favors the party's favorite ghoul joins. At 6 favors they reveal the door behind Captain Conroy in 33 FUNGUS and discuss their trauma. At 7 favors Gerdith gives her crysmere blade and they reveal Shawson's prison and magic item, though they will not help kill him.
