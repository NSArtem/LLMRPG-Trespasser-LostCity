# The Raid

## Operational fields

### Text

A decade ago, the Twelve-Fingered Hand, a militia and brotherhood of knights closely aligned with the White Temple, attacked the Red Temple and attempted to infiltrate through the crypts. The Hand failed; many were killed, many were entombed, and some are now ghouls.
