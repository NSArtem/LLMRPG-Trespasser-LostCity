# Captain Conroy's Fate and Last Order

## Operational fields

### Text

Captain Conroy remained with the ghouls for their first year, then became the fungal remains in 33 FUNGUS. Disgusted by ghoul hunger, he prayed to Shendormu and received this release. His last order was that his soldiers destroy themselves; some obeyed, and those who did not became the surviving ghouls.
