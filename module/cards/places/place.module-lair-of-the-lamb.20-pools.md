# 20 POOLS

## First impression

Four irregular pools, one each of pink, yellow, milky, and black liquid, occupy the room. A woman with lobster claws and a third eye is in the pink pool.

## Contents

- Four irregular pools of different colors: pink, yellow, milky, and black.
- A woman with lobster claws and a third eye in the pink pool.
- Occupant: Vandress, the eldest priestess, in the pink pool.

## Discoverable


## Hidden

- The pools are repositories for the more exotic types of waste produced by the Lamb.
- The pink pool is identical to the pink pools in area 19.

## Triggers


## Hazards

- The pink pool causes the same compulsion as the pink pools in area 19.
- The milky pool is acid (1d6 diminishing).
- Drinking from the black pool causes 1d6 rounds of vomiting heavier-than-air black smoke.

## Resources

- Yellow liquid light shines as brightly as daylight for 3 rounds when shaken or splashed; it works once.
