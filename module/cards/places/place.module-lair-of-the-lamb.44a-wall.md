# 44A WALL

## First impression

Waist-high water covers the area. The western brick wall looks newer than the surrounding dungeon masonry, and faint splashing can be heard behind it.

## Contents

- A more-modern brick wall on the west side.
- Waist-high water.

## Discoverable

- **Investigate the brick wall.** — The brick wall has a loose brick.
- **Remove the loose brick.** — Fresh air can be smelled through the wall.
- **Open the wall enough to shine torchlight through it.** — Beyond the wall is a huge room where water recedes into darkness; two huge water-stained pillars stand at the edge of torchlight.

## Hidden

- Noisy wall-breaking attracts every available ghoul, the apparatus, and the spider crab that is still alive and able to reach this location; each arrives in 5+1d4 minutes.
- The Lamb also hears the vibrations, arrives in 5+1d4 minutes, and waits on the bottom of 44 CISTERN near the mouth of the underwater tunnel.
- If multiple attracted parties arrive at the same time, violence will probably break out atop the stairs.

## Triggers

- Breaking down the wall is very noisy and initiates delayed arrivals from nearby threats.

## Hazards

- Noisy demolition can draw several hostile parties and the Lamb.

## Resources

- The loose brick provides a starting point for opening the wall.
- A couple of suitable tools allow the opening to be enlarged on a fixed timetable.
