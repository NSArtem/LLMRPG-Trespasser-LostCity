# 25E CRYPT

## First impression

A waxing-gibbous-moon motif marks this crypt, where a stone sarcophagus rests in the center.

## Contents

- A waxing gibbous moon motif.
- A central stone sarcophagus.

## Discoverable

- **Open the stone sarcophagus.** — The sarcophagus contains 7 mummified torsos, a cluster of large jelly-like eggs, and a fist-sized hole where something burrowed in.

## Hidden

- If kept near body heat or food, the eggs hatch into 1d6 slobberworms in 24 hours.

## Triggers

- Bringing a light source into the room causes the ghouls in 27 BALLISTA to notice it.

## Hazards

- The eggs can hatch into 1d6 slobberworms after 24 hours near body heat or food.
