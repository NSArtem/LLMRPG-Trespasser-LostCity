# 21 HEAVY DOORS

## First impression

A pair of heavy iron doors is held closed by a 10' heavy bar and chains; chatter and laughter are audible from Jasper and Luntz on the far side.

## Contents

- A 10' heavy bar.
- A set of chains.
- Occupant: Jasper and Luntz are on the far side when the doors are first approached.

## Discoverable


## Hidden

- Removing the bar grants a Milestone only if the Lamb has not already been killed.
- Jasper and Luntz intend to feign friendship and eat the party when they get an opportunity.

## Triggers

- Fumbling with the doors causes Jasper and Luntz to attempt an unconvincing friendly deception.
- If the party leaves and returns later, Jasper and Luntz instead wander the halls and may appear as random encounters.
