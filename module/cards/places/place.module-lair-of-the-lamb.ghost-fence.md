# Ghost Fence

## First impression

A line of 30 rotten severed heads divides the east and west sides of the room. Above them, 30 hands hang from a rope stretched north to south.

## Contents

- Thirty severed heads arranged as a barrier, alternating the directions they face; their yellow eyes look wet and alive.
- Thirty hands dangling directly above the heads from a 30-foot rope held by two iron spikes between the north and south walls.
- The rope and hands are entirely non-magical.

## Discoverable

- **Move at least one head out of formation and observe the result.** — Moving any head out of the specific formation ends the fence's effects; returning the heads to formation restores them.
- **Remove and later restore the heads in their specific formation.** — The heads can be moved elsewhere and reassembled to recreate the effect.

## Hidden

- Touching the heads or passing over them kills the nearest living thing with no Save.

## Triggers

- The heads continuously stare at the nearest living or undead creature and make that target chatter its teeth.
- Touching a head or passing over the formation triggers instant death for the nearest living thing.
- Restoring the specific head formation reactivates the effects.

## Hazards

- The nearest targeted creature chatters its teeth uncontrollably.
- Touching or crossing over the heads can kill the nearest living thing with no Save.
