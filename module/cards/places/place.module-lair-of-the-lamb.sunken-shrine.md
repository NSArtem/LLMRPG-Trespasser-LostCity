# Sunken Shrine

## First impression

A shrine chamber lies under 10 feet of water, with several submerged stone and metal forms visible below the surface.

## Contents

- Water 10 feet deep.
- Occupant: An ancient apparatus of anguish disguised externally as a quadrupedal statue.

## Discoverable

- **Look carefully into the water.** — Four stone pews, a rusted metal cage large enough to hold a person, a stone idol of Vandoh, and a quadrupedal statue lie beneath the water.
- **Examine the stone idol of Vandoh.** — The Vandoh statue is a polished marble muscular male in a toga, rendered in polygons with time-smoothed vertices; its platonic-solid head seems to have a different number of sides each time it is counted.

## Hidden

- The quadrupedal statue is an ancient apparatus of anguish made of pinkish-brown stone, grooves, and whorls.
- Four feet of water above the apparatus's head protects it from falling bricks.
- Two rounds after someone enters, the apparatus attacks and tries to exit the pit.
- The first person to speak or pray to the Vandoh statue has their left hand possessed.

## Triggers

- Two rounds after anyone enters the room, the ancient apparatus attempts to attack and leave the pit.
- The first person to speak or pray to the Vandoh statue triggers left-hand possession.

## Hazards

- The submerged ancient apparatus attacks intruders with its slam and Ancient Tone.
