# Collapse of 12 ABACUS MURAL

## What the players perceive

The sagging ceiling falls when its supporting pike is removed.

## Pressure and stakes

- A character can die while removing the pike.
- The room becomes impassable.
- The Lamb can be injured and temporarily trapped.

## Likely approaches


## Actor reactions


## Consequences

- Grabbing the pike and running requires a Movement check to avoid dying.
- The room becomes impassable.
- If the Lamb is beneath the collapse, it takes 3d6 damage, digs out in 30 minutes toward the north side, and is helpless for 1 round while emerging.
