# Collapse of 23 WEAK FLOOR

## What the players perceive

The sagging brick floor gives way in small patches when light objects land on it and can suddenly collapse completely under greater weight.

## Pressure and stakes

- Anyone on the brick floor may fall when the entire surface collapses.
- The unstable footing prevents effective force against the far door.

## Likely approaches

- Test the floor with thrown objects.
- Weaken the far door with acid or fire before attempting to break it.
- Use a ballista bolt to open the far door instantly.

## Actor reactions


## Consequences

- Every remaining brick floor tile falls away after the threshold is exceeded.
- The far door opens instantly if struck by a ballista bolt.
