# The Haunting of 9 FOUNTAIN

## What the players perceive

The first entrant collapses, gasps, and flops for 1 round while hallucinating that heavy armor is holding them underwater. The first person to touch the water is then shoved in without a save as a man’s death-gasps fill the air.

## Pressure and stakes

- A victim can be supernaturally held underwater.
- Destroying the skeleton can permanently curse the destroyer with fear of water.

## Likely approaches

- Remove the armored skeleton and pray over it.
- Destroy the armored skeleton.

## Actor reactions


## Consequences

- The first entrant hallucinates drowning for 1 round.
- The first person to touch the water is shoved in with no save and cannot get any part of their body out of the water; others experience normal water.
- Removing the skeleton and sincerely praying over it ends the haunting.
- Destroying the skeleton ends the haunting, but the destroyer must make a Charisma Save or become permanently afraid of water.
