# The Stone Egg Opens

## What the players perceive

The carved stone egg unfolds like a flower and the sound of screaming fills the air.

## Pressure and stakes

- The screaming provokes an Encounter check.
- Anyone who climbs inside can be sealed in and suffocated.

## Likely approaches

- Identify and touch all five egg-laying animal carvings simultaneously.

## Actor reactions


## Consequences

- The egg opens and exposes 600 silver shilavos, the Abacus of Vandoh, a perpetually screaming head, and a headless skeleton.
- A person who climbs inside is trapped until suffocation and transformed into a perpetually screaming head attached to a skeleton.
