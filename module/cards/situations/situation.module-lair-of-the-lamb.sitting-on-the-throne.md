# Sitting on the Throne

## What the players perceive

The seated character undergoes a bodily transformation and receives a vision of a purple book wrapped in fine copper chains.

## Pressure and stakes

- The character risks permanent obesity and a weaker claw, but may gain a stronger claw.

## Likely approaches

- Sit on the throne and make a Charisma Save.
- Avoid using the throne.

## Actor reactions


## Consequences

- Success: the right hand becomes a large lobster claw dealing 1d8.
- Failure: gain 3 Inventory Slots of fat and the right hand becomes a small lobster claw dealing 1d6.
- Regardless of result, receive a vision of a purple book wrapped in fine copper chains.
- Anyone with a lobster claw is Favored by Vandoh.
- Sitting on the throne counts as a Milestone.
