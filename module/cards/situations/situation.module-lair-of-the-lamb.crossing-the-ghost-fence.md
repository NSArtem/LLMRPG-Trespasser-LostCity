# Crossing the Ghost Fence

## What the players perceive

The wet-eyed severed heads stare at the nearest creature and chatter their teeth; the watched creature's own teeth chatter uncontrollably.

## Pressure and stakes

- Touching the heads or passing over them kills the nearest living thing with no Save.

## Likely approaches

- Move one or more heads out of formation.
- Use isopods like bowling balls to get past the barrier; the source notes that many solutions exist.

## Actor reactions


## Consequences

- Breaking the formation ends the effects.
- Restoring the formation reactivates the effects, even at a different location.
