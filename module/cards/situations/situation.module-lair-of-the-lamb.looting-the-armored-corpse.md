# Looting the Armored Corpse

## What the players perceive

An armored corpse lies 10 feet beyond a sealed portcullis, close enough to pull toward the bars but too large to pass through.

## Pressure and stakes

- The party can recover valuable equipment, but the breastplate and helmet cannot fit through the bars.

## Likely approaches

- Pull the corpse to the portcullis and remove items that fit.

## Actor reactions


## Consequences

- The sword, bow and arrows, signet ring, and Orb of Indulgence can be looted.
- The masterwork breastplate and helmet are too large to pass through the portcullis.
