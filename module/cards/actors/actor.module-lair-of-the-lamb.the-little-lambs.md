# The Little Lambs

## Appearance

## Role

Three offspring that emerge from the Lamb’s corpse and hunt as ceiling-climbing predators.

## Goals


## Behavior and reactions

- If the Lamb is killed but its corpse is not utterly destroyed, they wait quietly and then chew out of the corpse.
- They will not swim out to the cistern.
- They have a 2-in-6 chance to ambush from the ceiling and grapple only during the surprise round.
- **Bright light, fire, or burning oil is nearby.** — They avoid approaching within 10 feet of burning oil.

## Relationships


## Capabilities and mechanics

- Lvl 2; Def chain; Bite 1d8; Move human; Climb as slug; Str 5; Int 3; Dis hungry.
- Immune to acid.
- Will not approach within 10 feet of burning oil because of bright light and fire.
