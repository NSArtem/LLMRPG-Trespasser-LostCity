# The Lamb

## Appearance

## Role

The creature that hunts entrants in the cistern and can be fought, lured, ensnared, or killed.

## Goals


## Behavior and reactions

- When someone enters the center of 44 CISTERN, it starts swimming toward them.
- One round after beginning its approach, it urinates in anticipation and fills the air with an ammonia stink.
- On the following round, it loses patience and begins swimming on the surface.
- When noisy wall-breaking vibrations reach it, it arrives in 5+1d4 minutes and waits on the cistern bottom near the mouth of the underwater tunnel.
- It rests in 8 PIT 2-in-6 of the time.
- **A person enters the central 50-foot-by-50-foot area of 44 CISTERN.** — The Lamb starts swimming toward that person.
- **One round passes after the Lamb starts approaching.** — It urinates in anticipation, filling the air with the stink of ammonia.
- **Another round passes after the ammonia warning.** — It loses patience and swims on the surface.
- **The wall at 44A is broken down noisily.** — It arrives in 5+1d4 minutes, then waits on the bottom near the underwater tunnel mouth.
- **The party fishes for it with the hook hand and acceptable bait for the first time.** — The attempt works automatically.
