# Jasper

## Appearance

## Role

One of the five trapped second-floor ghouls; he wanders with Luntz while pretending to be a gentleman and is associated with theater and politics.

## Goals

- Satisfy his hunger for human meat.
- Gain the party's respect through a better performance than his own show in 29 STAGE.

## Behavior and reactions

- Wanders the halls with Luntz, pretending they are gentlemen returning from fine dining and entertainment.
- At 21 HEAVY DOORS, speaks to the door as though it were a party host withholding the best brandy.
- When hungry, openly talks about hunger and performs friendship unconvincingly.
- **The party fumbles with 21 HEAVY DOORS.** — He and Luntz feign friendship while intending to eat the party at the first opportunity.
- **The party offers dead bodies elsewhere as food.** — He and Luntz pretend to accept, then circle back and attack shortly afterward.
- **The party leaves and later returns.** — He and Luntz are wandering the halls and can appear on the Random Encounter table.
