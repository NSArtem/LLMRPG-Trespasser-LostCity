# Priest of the Pool

## Appearance

An old person wearing only a loincloth, smiling in sleep, occasionally twitching and groaning in a shallow pink pool.

## Role

One of three retired priests of Vandoh immersed in the pink pools of area 19; the three attack together with Vandress when any priest is touched.

## Goals


## Behavior and reactions

- When touched, the priest screams verbally and psychically.
- When any priest's scream awakens them, all three attack with Vandress.
- The three priests can speak only the words ‘lamb’, ‘blasphemy’, and ‘magenta’.
- **Any one of the priests is touched.** — The touched priest screams verbally and psychically; all four priests, including Vandress, awaken and attack.

## Relationships


## Capabilities and mechanics

- Lvl 1; Def none; Fists 1d3; Int 3; Dis guardian.
- Each of the three priests knows a different spell: sleep, desiccate, or emergency exit.

## Starting state

<!-- Source-stated starting state only. Current state belongs to the campaign checkpoint. -->

- One priest lies asleep in each of the three pink pools in area 19, smiling, twitching, and groaning.

## Hidden

- Immersion in the Lamb's psychotropic urine is the priests' reward, and they will remain there in ecstasy until they die hundreds of years from now.
