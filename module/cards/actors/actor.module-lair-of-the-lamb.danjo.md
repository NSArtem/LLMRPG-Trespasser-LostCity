# Danjo

## Appearance

A chicken vendor seated in the plaza beside the White Temple, visible through the fist-sized opening at the end of 15 CRACK.

## Role

An extortionate outside contact and merchant who can sell or fetch mundane goods.

## Goals

- Extract as much payment or leverage as possible from the party.

## Behavior and reactions

- Threatens to tell the temple guards unless given a reason not to.
- After the extortion issue is handled, sells mundane items at the highest price he can obtain, never below 20 times market value.
- Will run across town to purchase requested goods, taking 1-3 hours depending on the item.
- **The party first contacts him through the crack.** — He extorts them by threatening to inform the temple guards.
