# Vandress

## Appearance

A woman with lobster claws and a third eye.

## Role

The eldest priestess, immersed in the pink pool in area 20; unlike her peers, she retains full mental capacity and can parlay or pursue the party.

## Goals

- Gather information from the party.
- Drive the party off.
- Find the Lamb.
- Hunt the party with the Lamb's assistance.

## Behavior and reactions

- She is smart enough to parlay if the need arises.
- She prefers to gather information and drive the party off before finding the Lamb and hunting the party with its assistance.
- If any priest is touched, she awakens with the other priests and attacks.
- **The need arises to avoid or delay a direct confrontation.** — Vandress may parlay.
- **Any priest is touched.** — Vandress awakens and attacks with the other three priests.

## Relationships

- **The Lamb** (`actor.module-lair-of-the-lamb.the-lamb-c0865d75`) — She intends to find the Lamb and use its assistance to hunt the party.

## Capabilities and mechanics

- Lvl 2; Def none; Claw 1d8; Int 7; Dis scheming.
- Spells: confusion, cone of mutilation.
- Retains her full mental capacity.
