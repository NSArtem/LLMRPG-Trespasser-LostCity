# Shawson

## Appearance

A monstrous, completely feral ghoul; when briefly seen from 37 CHEWED BONES, only his yellow eyes are visible before he growls and retreats.

## Role

A ghoul who lost his mind, was imprisoned by the other ghouls in 39 STONE EGG, and waits to ambush anyone opening the high secret passage from 37 CHEWED BONES.

## Goals

- Grab and eat the first person who climbs into the secret opening.

## Behavior and reactions

- Waits beside the opening after hearing the secret door open.
- Growls and retreats into 39 STONE EGG if exposed by torchlight from a chair in 37 CHEWED BONES.
- **The first person climbs into the opened secret passage from 37 CHEWED BONES.** — Shawson surprises them, grabs them, and drags them into 39 STONE EGG to eat them.
- **Torchlight from a chair in the middle of 37 CHEWED BONES catches his eyes.** — He growls and retreats into 39 STONE EGG.
