# Three Armored Isopods

## Appearance

Three armored isopods forage in the room.

## Role

Non-aggressive metal-eating creatures in 32 ISOPODS.

## Goals


## Behavior and reactions

- Ignore the party while foraging.
- Roll into balls if poked.
- Attempt to fight back if damaged.
- Eat metal by licking it and later nibbling away the rust.
- **Poked.** — Rolls into a ball.
- **Damaged.** — Attempts to fight back.

## Relationships


## Capabilities and mechanics

- Lvl 1; Def plate; Bite 1d6; Move slow; Int 1; Dis leave me alone.
- Defense Curl: while curled, an armored isopod cannot be damaged by anything less than a sledgehammer or another 2H bludgeon.
- Their shells are only a few millimeters thick and are made from an extremely strong biometallic matrix.
