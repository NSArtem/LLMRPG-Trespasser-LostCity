# Gerdith

## Appearance

## Role

An armored ghoul who keeps watch over the company ballista in 27 BALLISTA, patrols nearby rooms, and plays tactical miniature games.

## Goals

- Convince herself that she is still on patrol.
- Eat intruders.
- Gain respect through a miniature wargame in 28 GAMES.

## Behavior and reactions

- Marches constantly around 27 BALLISTA and denies that she is a ghoul.
- Is alert to combat, a portcullis winch, a dropped sarcophagus lid, and light in the rooms she can see.
- Once alerted, loudly orders Molina to accompany her while she investigates.
- Runs on all fours and behaves intelligently and ravenously.
- **Combat, a portcullis winch, a dropped sarcophagus lid, or torchlight entering a visible room alerts her.** — She orders Molina to accompany her and investigates.

## Relationships

- **Molina** (`actor.module-lair-of-the-lamb.molina`) — Fellow armored ghoul and ballista guard whom she commands during investigations.
- **Captain Conroy** (`actor.module-lair-of-the-lamb.captain-conroy`) — Former soldier under Captain Conroy; the ballista is named Conroy.

## Capabilities and mechanics

- Lvl 2; Def plate; Halberd 1d8; Move as human; Int 5; Str 7; Dis hungry.
- Skilled in military trivia and tactical board games.
- Can hear all rooms between 25 CRYPT and 29 STAGE and can see to 29 STAGE and 25G CRYPT.
