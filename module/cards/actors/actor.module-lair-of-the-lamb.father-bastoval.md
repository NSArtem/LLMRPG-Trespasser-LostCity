# Father Bastoval

## Appearance

## Role

The Lamb’s father and a Nemesis who enters the dungeon with two elderly bodyguards after the Lamb is killed.

## Goals


## Behavior and reactions

- Two hours after the Lamb is killed, he enters the dungeon with his bodyguards to check on it.
- If the Lamb is dead, he hunts the Lamb’s murderers.

## Relationships

- **Mino** (`actor.module-lair-of-the-lamb.mino`) — Bodyguard accompanying Bastoval.
- **Bilosh** (`actor.module-lair-of-the-lamb.bilosh`) — Bodyguard accompanying Bastoval.

## Capabilities and mechanics

- Lvl 3; Def chain; Warfork 1d8; Dis wrathful.
- Spells: delay, haste, scry.
- As a Nemesis, each time he kills a PC he gains a level and is thereafter introduced as “Bastoval, Slayer of X”.
