# Luntz

## Appearance

## Role

One of the five trapped second-floor ghouls; he wanders with Jasper while pretending to be a gentleman and is associated with cooking, butchery, and lewd jokes.

## Goals

- Satisfy his hunger for human meat.

## Behavior and reactions

- Wanders the halls with Jasper, pretending they are gentlemen returning from fine dining and entertainment.
- When hungry, openly talks about hunger and performs friendship unconvincingly.
- **The party fumbles with 21 HEAVY DOORS.** — He and Jasper feign friendship while intending to eat the party at the first opportunity.
- **The party offers dead bodies elsewhere as food.** — He and Jasper pretend to accept, then circle back and attack shortly afterward.
- **The party leaves and later returns.** — He and Jasper are wandering the halls and can appear on the Random Encounter table.
