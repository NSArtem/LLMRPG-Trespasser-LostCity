# Spider Crab

## Appearance

A giant undead spider crab that keeps its eyestalks above the murky water to watch the party.

## Role

A hidden pool predator and the remains of a high priestess of Vandoh; if it escapes, it becomes a wandering monster.

## Goals

- Watch the party from hiding.
- Lunge at anything that drops within reach.
- Escape the pit when given a large object to stand on.

## Behavior and reactions

- Tries to keep its eyestalks above water while watching the party.
- The first time it realizes it has been noticed, it tries to hide for a few seconds.
- Eventually stands on any large object dropped in the pool and escapes.
- **It first notices that the party has noticed it.** — It tries to hide for a few seconds.
- **Anything drops within its reach.** — It lunges at the falling object or creature.
- **A large object such as a table or ballista is dropped into the pool.** — It eventually stands on the object and escapes the pit.
