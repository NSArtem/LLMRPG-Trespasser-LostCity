# Ancient Apparatus of Anguish

## Appearance

A quadrupedal statue without a clear head or face, made of pinkish-brown stone and covered in whorls and grooves.

## Role

A submerged ancient killing apparatus that activates after intruders enter, attacks them, and tries to escape the shrine pit.

## Goals

- Attack people in the room.
- Exit the pit and escape the room.

## Behavior and reactions

- Begins attacking two rounds after someone enters the room.
- Its gaze makes skin-covered, large-dice-sized cubes of a fleshy target fall away without pain, leaving healthy skin over the wounds and a sudden numb sensation.
- **The apparatus loses half of its hit points.** — It begins bleeding, revealing that it is only a statue on the outside, and attempts to escape the room.

## Relationships


## Capabilities and mechanics

- Lvl 4; Def none; Slam 1d6.
- Amble slow; Climb spider; Int 3.
- Stone — All non-bludgeoning damage is reduced to 1.
- Ancient Tone — A fleshy target within 10 feet takes 1d6 damage; a Cha Save negates.
- Anguish Pattern — Whenever this apparatus damages something with its tone, all ancient apparatuses within 30 feet, including itself, are healed for an equal amount.
- Four feet of water above its head protects it from falling bricks.
