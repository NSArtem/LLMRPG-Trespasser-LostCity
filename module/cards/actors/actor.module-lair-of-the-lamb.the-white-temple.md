# The White Temple

## Appearance

## Role

One of the three great temples holding power in Lon Barago, worshipping Vandoh; its structure above the cistern receives water from the noria, and it may retaliate if its godling is expelled into its drinking fountain.

## Goals


## Behavior and reactions

- It is corrupt and unsympathetic.
- **The Lamb is dragged up by the noria and viscerally ejected into the White Temple's drinking fountain.** — A swift and forceful response can be expected.

## Relationships

- **The Lamb** (`actor.module-lair-of-the-lamb.the-lamb`) — The White Temple regards the Lamb as its godling.
