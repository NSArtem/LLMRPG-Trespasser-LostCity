# The Lamb

## Appearance

A gigantic, hairless, obese cow-like creature with no obvious head; its neck ends in a drooping cowl of wrinkled flesh, from which it can extend a bloody horse-skull head with sunken eyes and a black tongue. It weighs about 2500 lbs, drags its belly, and reeks of blood and ammonia.

## Role

The dungeon's central predator and the White Temple's holy child: it guards and roams between its pit and fountain, produces the waste stored in area 20, and is the creature Vandress intends to seek when hunting the party.

## Goals


## Behavior and reactions

- Mostly remains in 8 PIT and 9 FOUNTAIN.
- Its sight and hearing are slightly worse than an average human’s.
- It is not smart but is clever, learns from mistakes, and adopts better tactics with exposure.
- If it finishes eating or loses track of the party, it heads to 9 FOUNTAIN for a bath.
- **It is wounded or scared.** — It calls “Father! Father!” in increasingly loud tones; it can say nothing else.
- **People hide from it during the opening encounter.** — It devours one restrained human at random over 10 minutes and is not very alert while eating.
- **People flee noisily during the opening encounter.** — It attacks.
- **People leave slowly and quietly during the opening encounter.** — It does not notice them.
- **It has experienced 3 encounters with the party near 10 BONE PILE.** — It becomes cunning and begins blocking the crawl tunnel with bones.

## Relationships

- **The White Temple** (`actor.module-lair-of-the-lamb.the-white-temple`) — The White Temple keeps the Lamb as one of its secrets.
- **Vandress** (`actor.module-lair-of-the-lamb.vandress`) — Vandress intends to find the Lamb and hunt the party with its assistance.

## Capabilities and mechanics

- Lvl 6; Def chain; Bite/Crush 1d8/1d18; Move slow; Climb slug; Str 9; Int 3; Dis hungry.
- Immune to acid.
- Torches and lanterns make it hesitate, but only a large fire scares it away.
- If people try to run past it in a hallway or room, it makes both attacks.
- Can climb out of 8 PIT in 2 rounds.
- Carries 52 Nascent Lambfruit and 6 Immature Lambfruit.
- Produces the exotic types of waste stored in the four pools of area 20.
