# Lantern Worm

## Appearance

A 15' long creature, glossy like black rubber; when coiled it resembles a huge taut black rubber hose, and its searchlight is visible before the worm.

## Role

A hungry roaming or quiescent dungeon predator that seeks and consumes light sources.

## Goals


## Behavior and reactions

- A coiled lantern worm is quiescent until touched or until light is brought near.
- It retreats after it has eaten all available light sources.

## Relationships


## Capabilities and mechanics

- Lvl 4; Def leather; Slam 1d6; Crawl slow; Int 2; Dis hungry.
- Eat Light: a targeted light source explodes and is destroyed; the worm eats the light and becomes brighter. The holder takes 1d6 fire damage unless a Wis Save negates it and is automatically blinded; everyone else is blinded on a failed Wis Save. A Con check each turn ends the blindness.
- Lantern: sheds light in a narrow 60' cone. Whoever it looks at gets -4 to hit, also applying to adjacent people.
- Stonemeld: can become a liquid and flow through stone, appearing as a wet dark stain.
