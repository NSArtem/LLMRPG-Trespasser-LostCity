# Hirelings

## Operational fields

### Text

Henchmen cost 1s per day or half a share of loot, whichever is higher. They have 10 inventory slots, willingly carry torches, accompany the party through the dungeon, and neither take risks nor fight. Mercenaries cost 10s per day or a full loot share, whichever is higher, and stay near their employer. They accept risks including combat provided they are not asked to take more risk than everyone else. Mercenaries do not take separate combat turns: an adjacent mercenary grants the employer attack and damage bonuses. A level 1 fighter grants +2 Attack and +2 Damage and has a 50% chance to die instead when the employer would take lethal physical damage. Local-tavern mercenaries are level 1 fighters by default. A level 2 fighter grants +3 Attack and +3 Damage and can take two hits before dying. A level 1 thief grants +1 Attack and +1 Damage and can be persuaded to pick locks and scout rooms. A level 0 mercenary grants +1 Attack and +1 Damage. Every mercenary after the first contributes only +1 Attack.
