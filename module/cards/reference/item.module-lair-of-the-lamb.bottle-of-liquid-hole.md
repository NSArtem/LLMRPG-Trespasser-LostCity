# Liquid Hole

## Operational fields

### Text

A vial of utterly black oil that bends light toward itself, produces a lensing effect in the surrounding air, and has no smell. Tasting it creates a large piercing-like hole in the tongue. Applied to a surface, it creates a hole up to 6 inches wide and 5 feet deep, never wider than half the object; created holes cannot intersect. Applied to a portcullis, it can make the bars resemble Swiss cheese without breaking them. Consuming it creates a hole in the abdomen and causes the consumer's guts to fall out.
