# Preparation

## Operational fields

### Text

In town, the Thief may obtain an Unmarked Package for 10s from an unspecified black market or shady alley. When opened or removed from the Thief's possession, it becomes a declared mundane item worth no more than 10s, representing prior foresight and effectively delivering the needed tool to the current location.
