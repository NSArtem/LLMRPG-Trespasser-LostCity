# Sunken Gallery Poison Gas

## Operational fields

### Text

When a poison-filled coffer is opened, anyone in front must make a Dex save or be poisoned for 1d6 damage per round until a Con check succeeds. A hooked pole or opening from the side grants +4 to the Dex check. The gas then fills the room; characters who flee immediately are not poisoned, and the gas takes 10 minutes to settle.
