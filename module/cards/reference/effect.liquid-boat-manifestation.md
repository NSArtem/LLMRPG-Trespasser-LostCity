# Liquid Boat Manifestation

## Operational fields

### Text

Dispensed liquid becomes a boat according to quantity: a drop makes a toy boat; one quarter of the vial makes a 4-person rowboat; one half makes an 8-person small sloop; and the entire vial makes a full-size ship for 16. Insufficient space produces a deformed nonsense boat.
