# Vandoh’s Favor Progression

## Operational fields

### Text

Favor from Vandoh grants exactly one use of clairvoyance and leaves a vertical forehead wrinkle after a temporary visible third eye vanishes. On the first morning after leaving the dungeon, one of many origami-bird letters invites the recipient to the White Temple for an optional priesthood and access to level as a cleric of Vandoh. Prompt arrival preserves the offer and impresses the priests; delay or nonappearance makes them rescind it and become wrathful. The clerics have no way to identify the recipient’s location directly.
