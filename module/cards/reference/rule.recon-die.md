# The Recon Die

## Operational fields

### Text

The Recon Die is a blue d6 and succeeds on a 1. On an Active or Passive Encounter, success grants Surprise, allowing the party either to engage with surprise or avoid the encounter; the DM may also let the party overhear useful information. If no Active or Passive Encounter occurs, a successful Recon finds traces of a randomly rolled encounter, such as slime, hair, feces, graffiti, smells, or noises. With only candlelight, replace the Recon Die with an Ambush Die that surprises the party on a 1. With no light source, the party is ambushed on 1-2 on a d6.
