# Magic Dice Doubles and Triples

## Operational fields

### Text

Rolling doubles or triples on MD causes harm to the caster. The page points to GLOG: Wizards for full rules and offers a simplified option: doubles deal 1d6 damage and triples deal 3d6 damage.
