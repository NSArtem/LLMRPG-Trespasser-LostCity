# Davok’s Geased Thrall

## Operational fields

### Text

Davok demands to be carried out of the dungeon and casts geas until someone complies. The geased thrall remains a playable PC but is utterly dedicated to serving Davok, will not tolerate words or actions against him, and calls Davok’s name when pressed. Once carried, Davok appears to sleep while searching the ether for fragments of his body.
