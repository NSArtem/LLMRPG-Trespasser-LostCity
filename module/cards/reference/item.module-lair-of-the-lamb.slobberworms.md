# Slobberworms

## Operational fields

### Text

Harmless, disgusting creatures resembling fat maggots the size of mice. When fed organic material, they produce flatus, more slobberworms, and a large amount of extremely slippery slime equivalent to grease whenever squeezed. They can eat anything a brave pig could eat, and with enough food their population can double every day.
