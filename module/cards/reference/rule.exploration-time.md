# Exploration Time

## Operational fields

### Text

The DM tracks time because it controls light depletion and random encounters. An exploration turn is 10 minutes. Dungeoneering actions usually take 1 exploration turn, including searching a room or body, prying a torch sconce from a wall, and passing through a room for the first time. This pace assumes cautious dungeon movement: whispering, examining details, testing each step, and listening.
