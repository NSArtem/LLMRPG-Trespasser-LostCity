# Favored by Vandoh

## Operational fields

### Text

A character with a lobster claw is Favored by Vandoh. If all seven mummies are reassembled and returned, or close enough, the character who took the most initiative becomes Favored by Vandoh. If all ghouls are killed, the character whose left hand was possessed becomes Favored by Vandoh; Appendix D gives the full effect.
