# Barbarian

## Operational fields

### Text

Anyone can become a Barbarian without first unlocking the class through play. Each level of Barbarian grants +2 Loincloth HP. Advancement grants Rage at A, Firewater at B, Thews 1/day at C, and Rampage plus Thews 2/day at D.
