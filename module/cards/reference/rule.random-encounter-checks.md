# Random Encounter Checks

## Operational fields

### Text

Each area has a local Encounter Table that determines who may appear. Every 30 minutes, roll the yellow Encounter Die and blue Recon Die simultaneously. The Encounter Die determines whether the result is Active, Passive, Indirect, or Depletion. An Active Encounter forces engagement, usually through a hostile creature. A Passive Encounter allows the party to choose whether to engage. An Indirect Encounter cannot be directly engaged but may provide information or establish the mood of the location.
