# Shendormu’s Favor

## Operational fields

### Text

The favored character immediately receives a vision of a huge green fungal sun as the only illumination, while the character’s body becomes spores and a thousand distinct emotions are felt at once. The character gains one and only one casting of control emotions, and their eyes permanently turn green. On the first morning after leaving the dungeon, only that character sees a green glow beckoning toward the Many-Layered Hall. There the character is recognized as favored and offered a priesthood and the ability to level as a cleric of Shendormu, without any obligation to accept.
