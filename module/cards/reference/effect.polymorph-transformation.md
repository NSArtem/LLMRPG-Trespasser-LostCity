# Polymorph Transformation

## Operational fields

### Text

For [sum] rounds, the target is transformed according to one chosen adjective per invested MD: small, big, dangerous, harmless, mobile, or slow. Repeating big or dangerous twice intensifies it to very big or very dangerous; slow chosen three times can make the creature immobile. Without selected changes, the form is human-sized, self-defending, and roughly human-mobile. Investing 4 MD makes the transformation permanent. A successful save negates it.
