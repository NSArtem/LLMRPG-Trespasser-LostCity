# Skull of Davok

## Operational fields

### Text

The lead-and-iron chest in 4 CHESTS contains only the Skull of Davok, which escapes when the chest is opened. The remnant of the demon Davok resembles a deformed elephant skull. Once freed, it tries once per round to absorb flesh from one person within 30 feet (Cha Save or die), regrows much of its head and voice after absorbing flesh, demands to be carried out, and casts geas until obeyed. It can call a carpet of vermin to carry it at a crawl; if it wanders this way, add Davok to the Random Encounter Table in place of Atmospheric Encounters for rooms 1–20. It awakens when touched or when its name is called, casts ignite and polymorph with 3 MD, is stunned for 1 minute by 10 damage in one round while reconstructing, cannot be destroyed in this dungeon, and becomes inert if returned to its lead-lined chest. Once free it searches for its missing body parts and forces its loyal thrall into Davok's service.
