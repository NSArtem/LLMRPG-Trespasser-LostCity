# Noisy 44A Breach Attracts Threats

## Operational fields

### Text

Breaking down the 44A wall is very noisy. Every available ghoul, the apparatus, and the spider crab that is still alive and able to reach the location arrives in 5+1d4 minutes. The Lamb also arrives in 5+1d4 minutes and waits on the bottom of 44 CISTERN near the underwater tunnel mouth.
