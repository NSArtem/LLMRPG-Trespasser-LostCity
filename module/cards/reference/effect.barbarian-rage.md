# Rage

## Operational fields

### Text

While raging, the Barbarian gains +1 Attack and +1 Damage and is immune to pain and fear. The Barbarian cannot do anything defensive, curative, or cooperative; mostly only attacks in different ways are possible, and any spell cast must be damaging.
