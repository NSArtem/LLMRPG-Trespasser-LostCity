# Vandoh’s Favor

## Operational fields

### Text

The favored character is immediately transformed: a visible third eye with a white sclera and still whiter iris opens on the forehead, the world becomes translucent except for the apex of the White Temple, and the character feels drawn there. The character gains one and only one casting of clairvoyance. The third eye then vanishes, leaving a vertical forehead wrinkle. On the first morning after leaving the dungeon, an origami bird lands and becomes an inert letter from Vandoh’s priests. The letter explains that the character should have been recruited rather than offered to the Lamb and invites them to the White Temple for an optional priesthood and access to level as a cleric of Vandoh. The priests cannot locate the character and send many birds. Prompt arrival produces no hard feelings and impresses the priests; delay or failure to appear causes them to rescind the offer and become wrathful.
