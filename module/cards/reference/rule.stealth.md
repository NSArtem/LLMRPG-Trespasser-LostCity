# Stealth

## Operational fields

### Text

A Stealth check is a difficult Dex check with a baseline DC of 20. The baseline scenario is approaching someone facing away without being heard and silently cutting their throat; adjust the DC for easier or harder circumstances. Failure means the character is spotted. Stealth is distinct from hiding: anyone can hide by describing how to the DM.
