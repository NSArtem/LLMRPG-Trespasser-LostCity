# Polymorph

## Operational fields

### Text

Range 30 feet; target creature; duration [sum] rounds. The target becomes a creature that may be small, big, dangerous, harmless, mobile, or slow. Choose one adjective for each MD invested. An adjective may be chosen twice to make the result very big or very dangerous; choosing slow three times can make it immobile. The default result is human-sized, able to defend itself, and about as mobile as a human, such as a goat. Investing 4 MD makes the transformation permanent. A save negates the spell. Splittable.
