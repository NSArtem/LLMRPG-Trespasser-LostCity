# Black Iron Spellbook Instruction

## Operational fields

### Text

Possession and study of the book permit a character to begin learning Cheoxic, provide the spells speak with dead and fear, and unlock leveling as a Necromancer. Reaching Necromancer Level 1 causes the lich Shadrakul to ask the character to become her apprentice.
