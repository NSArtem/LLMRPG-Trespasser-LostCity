# Leveling Up

## Operational fields

### Text

A character levels whenever the party obtains a Treasure or reaches a Milestone, but not during combat. The lowest-level PC gains the level; break ties randomly. Every Treasure or Milestone also gives at least one person a Title chosen through discussion among the players and DM. At first level, HP becomes equal to Con, Atk becomes 1, and the player chooses a stat and rolls 1d6+1d4; if the roll exceeds that stat, it rises by 1. The character also chooses Fighter, Barbarian, Thief, or Acrobat. Additional classes are unlocked through play; see Appendix E.
