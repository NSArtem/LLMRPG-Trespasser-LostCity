# Delay

## Operational fields

### Text

Range 30 feet; target 1 effect; duration [dice] rounds. Delays an effect—something happening to one object because of an interaction with another object or spell. It can delay incoming damage or prevent a sword from breaking until later. It may be cast as a reaction outside the caster's turn and takes no time. The spell can create paradoxes, which the GM is encouraged to resolve in the way most painful to the caster.
