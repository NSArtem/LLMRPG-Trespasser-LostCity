# Setting the Stage

## Operational fields

### Text

An encounter entry begins with a general encounter, such as 1d6 goblins. If a Unique encounter is killed, it never appears again in that area. A second sentence may give a specific scene; use it the first time the encounter is rolled unless the DM prefers an original setup. On later rolls, create a new scene or build from the prior one. Place enemies where their arrival makes the most sense; randomly choose among equally plausible origins. Encounter placement must respect established topology and changes: enemies cannot emerge from an impossible dead end, and collapsing 12 ABACUS MURAL can make the south side safe from the Lamb if the Lamb remains trapped to the north.
