# Dessicate

## Operational fields

### Text

Range 30 feet; target object; duration 0. Evaporates 1 gallon of liquid, multiplied by 10 for every MD beyond the first. Alternatively, it deals [sum] damage to an animal foe, with a Cha Save for half. Drier targets take less or no damage; wetter targets, such as oozes, take more.
