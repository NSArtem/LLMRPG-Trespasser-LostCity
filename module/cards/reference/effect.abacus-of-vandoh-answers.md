# Abacus of Vandoh Answers

## Operational fields

### Text

Up to three times per day, the abacus can answer a qualifying question with an integer from 2 through 9. A secret d100 roll determines the result: 11–100 answers honestly, 2–9 answers with the rolled number, and 1 or 10 produces no response. Questions are limited to obdurate physical things within 100 feet, may contain no more than 12 words, and cannot depend on names, mind-reading, or reliable distinction between humans.
