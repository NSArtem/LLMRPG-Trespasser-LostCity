# Geas Mutual Quest Binding

## Operational fields

### Text

For [dice] weeks, the target must pursue a caster-chosen quest with a decent chance of success, while the caster must help. Neither participant may directly harm the other. Completion compels the caster to provide a proper reward. A successful save negates the binding.
