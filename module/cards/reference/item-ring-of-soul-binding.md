A magical fairy engagement ring bestowed by Snowfall-at-Dusk on Sir Chyde: a bronze band set with a moonstone, with fittings shaped like woven branches. It binds their souls together for eternity, allows communion across imprisonment and death, and binds Sir Chyde's ghost to it. The full item is referenced on page 24 of the source adventure.
