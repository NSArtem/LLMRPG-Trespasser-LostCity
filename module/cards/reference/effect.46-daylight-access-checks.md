# Reaching the Stairs at 46 DAYLIGHT

## Operational fields

### Text

Reaching the stone stairs 20 feet overhead requires either a successful grappling-hook Attack roll against AC 15 or two Movement checks to climb the slick walls, one check for each 10 feet.
