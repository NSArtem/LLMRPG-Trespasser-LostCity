# Liquid Hole Surface Perforation

## Operational fields

### Text

Applied to a surface, the oil creates a hole up to 6 inches wide and 5 feet deep. The hole cannot be wider than half the object, and separate holes cannot intersect; therefore perforating portcullis bars does not itself break them.
