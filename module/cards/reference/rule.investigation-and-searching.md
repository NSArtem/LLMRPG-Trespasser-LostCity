# Investigation and Searching

## Operational fields

### Text

Investigation means a player specifically examines a feature or interacts with an object, such as opening a drawer, and usually takes negligible time. Searching means the party spends 10 minutes examining a room more closely, such as sifting through a bone pile. Anything discoverable through Investigation can also be found by Searching.
