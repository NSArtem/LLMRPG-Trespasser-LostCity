# Monster Spell Casting and Magic Dice Recovery

## Operational fields

### Text

To cast, a monster chooses a spell, a target, and how many MD to invest, then rolls those MD. Dice showing 1, 2, or 3 return to the mana pool for later turns; dice showing 4, 5, or 6 are expended until the next day.
