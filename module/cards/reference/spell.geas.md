# Geas

## Operational fields

### Text

Range 30 feet; target person; duration [dice] weeks. The target is bound to complete a quest chosen by the caster, and the caster is bound to help with that quest. Neither may directly harm the other. The quest must have a decent chance of success. When it is completed, the caster is compelled to reward the target properly. A save negates the spell.
