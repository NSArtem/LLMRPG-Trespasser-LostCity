# Soul Severed by the Shroud Guillotine

## Operational fields

### Text

When the winched guillotine falls on someone whose head is in it, the victim's soul is severed and sent to room 99; the neck and head remain fully intact because there is no blade. A person without a soul is immune to mind reading, mind control, and emotions; does not detect as living, dead, or undead; sits down and stares at a wall whenever left alone; says they do not feel mindless but feel empty; and never levels up again.
