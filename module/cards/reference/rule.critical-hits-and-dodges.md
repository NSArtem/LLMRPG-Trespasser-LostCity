# Critical Hits and Dodges

## Operational fields

### Text

A PC critical hit deals double damage or regular damage plus a free combat maneuver. A PC critical dodge takes no damage and allows a free combat maneuver or attack. An enemy critical hit deals double damage and makes a random piece of the target's armor lose 1 Def. An enemy critical dodge grants the enemy a free attack and breaks the attacker's weapon, reducing its damage die by one step. A masterwork weapon must be broken twice before its die shrinks. Only magical foes can break magical arms and armor.
