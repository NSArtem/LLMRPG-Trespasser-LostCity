# Davok Vermin Transport

## Operational fields

### Text

With 1 hour of work, Davok can summon a carpet of vermin that carries the skull at a crawl. If he wanders the dungeon this way, he replaces Atmospheric Encounters on the Random Encounter Table for rooms 1–20.
