# Combat Maneuvers

## Operational fields

### Text

Combat maneuvers include actions such as tripping and shoving. First the enemy makes a free attack against the acting character; skip this step when the maneuver is the free maneuver granted by a critical hit. Then make an opposed Str or Dex check against DC 11 + the enemy's chosen Str or Dex, using whichever stats fit the maneuver. Success performs the maneuver.
