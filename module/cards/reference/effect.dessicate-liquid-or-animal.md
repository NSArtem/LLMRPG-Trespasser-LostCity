# Dessicate Liquid or Animal

## Operational fields

### Text

The spell either evaporates 1 gallon of liquid, with a ×10 multiplier for each MD after the first, or deals [sum] damage to an animal foe. A Cha Save halves animal damage; dryness reduces or eliminates damage, while wetness can increase it.
