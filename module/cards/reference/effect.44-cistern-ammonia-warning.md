# The Lamb's Ammonia Warning

## Operational fields

### Text

One round after the Lamb begins swimming toward someone in the center of 44 CISTERN, it urinates in anticipation and fills the air with an ammonia stink. On the following round it loses patience and begins swimming on the surface.
