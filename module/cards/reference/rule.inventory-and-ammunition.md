# Inventory and Ammunition

## Operational fields

### Text

Inventory slots equal Str plus the bonus from a container. A character without a container can carry only two things, one in each hand. A Burlap Sack gives +4 slots, a Backpack +7, and an Elfpack +10. One ration feeds the whole group. Bow ammunition is loosely tracked: mark one depletion after each encounter in which a bow is used; after three depletions, the character is out of ammunition.
