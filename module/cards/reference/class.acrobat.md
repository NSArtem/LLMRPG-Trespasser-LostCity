# Acrobat

## Operational fields

### Text

Anyone can become an Acrobat. Each Acrobat level grants +1 Move. Template A grants Dodge and Nah 1/day; Template B grants Tricky; Template C grants Cat Power and increases Nah to 2/day; Template D grants Roast the Oaf. Dodge gives +2 Defense per empty inventory slot, capped at 16 or 10 + Dex, whichever is lower, and does not stack with armor. Nah can declare once per day, or twice per day at Template C, that something physically dodgeable does not affect the Acrobat. Tricky makes a natural 20 attack deal double damage and grant a free combat maneuver; a natural 19 deals normal damage and grants a free combat maneuver. Cat Power treats falls as 20 feet shorter and causes cats to tend to like the Acrobat. Roast the Oaf lets the Acrobat force a larger enemy that misses with a melee attack to make another melee attack against a target in range, potentially itself. Two copies of Tricky from two Acrobat and two Fighter levels combine into Very Tricky.
