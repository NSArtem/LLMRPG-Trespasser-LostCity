# Ancient Tone

## Operational fields

### Text

A fleshy target within 10 feet takes 1d6 damage; a Cha Save negates. The apparatus's gaze makes parts of the target fall away as skin-covered cubes about the size of large dice. The resulting wounds are covered with healthy skin; there is no pain, only sudden numbness.
