# Pink Pool Compulsion

## Operational fields

### Text

Anyone tasting a pink pool is compelled to taste more and eventually lie down in it. No Save. Anyone who tastes it is compelled to do everything in their power to return. If forcibly removed, the effect lasts 10 minutes before fading from a compulsion to lingering dissatisfaction with all of life's other pleasures.
