# Dying

## Operational fields

### Text

A level 0 character dies upon taking any damage. Other characters convert damage beyond HP into Lethal Damage, fall unconscious, and start Dying. At the end of each round, a Dying character makes a Con check at DC 15 + current Lethal Damage. Reaching 10 Lethal Damage causes death. An ally can spend an action to Stabilize the character with an Int check against the same DC. Any amount of magical healing also Stabilizes. Stabilization wakes the character and allows normal actions, but HP remains 0 and Lethal Damage is unchanged. Resting in town while safe and fed removes 1 Lethal Damage.
