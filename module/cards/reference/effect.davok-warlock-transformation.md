# Davok Warlock Transformation

## Operational fields

### Text

Once Davok is free, his loyal thrall becomes a warlock in his service, by force if the thrall refuses. The rest of the party may also enter Davok’s service or depart; if they depart, the newly made warlock becomes an allied NPC in Lon Barago.
