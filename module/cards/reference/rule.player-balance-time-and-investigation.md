# Balance Time Pressure against Investigation

## Operational fields

### Text

Time is the enemy: torches burn down, the party has no food or water, and every 30 minutes carries a risk of a Random Encounter. Survival also requires taking time to investigate suspicious phenomena, so players must balance these competing needs.
