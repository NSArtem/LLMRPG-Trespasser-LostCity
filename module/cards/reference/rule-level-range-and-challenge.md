# Character Levels and Encounter Approach

- ID: `rule-level-range-and-challenge`
- Type: `rule`
- Source pages: 5

## Operational fields

### Text

The adventure is intended for 1st- to 3rd-level PCs. Encounters are not all balanced to the party's capabilities; play should favor discretion and the judicious use of stealth, parley, and trickery rather than blindly entering combat.
