# Alternate Level-Up Timing

## Operational fields

### Text

Instead of the Treasure/Milestone system, level everyone when they escape the dungeon. Because escape may take multiple sessions, the GM may instead level everyone at the halfway point when they open 21 HEAVY DOOR.
