# Purple Lotus Powder

## Operational fields

### Text

A purple pouch in 4 CHESTS and a small bag in 34 CACHE each contain 2 doses of purple lotus powder. When smoked, it causes 1d3 hours of sleep; on waking, the GM rapidly presents a fun dream, a dream of something that happened, a scary dream, and a dream of something likely to happen in random order, with at least one semi-useful. A smoker thereafter cannot sleep without more purple lotus powder unless they have remained sleepless for 3 nights.
