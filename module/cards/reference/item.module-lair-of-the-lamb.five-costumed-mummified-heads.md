# Five Costumed Mummified Heads

## Operational fields

### Text

Five mummified heads on 29 STAGE are costumed as a clown, king, priest, prostitute, and farmer. The first four face the farmer, who faces the audience. They are the missing heads for the seven-mummy reconstruction.
