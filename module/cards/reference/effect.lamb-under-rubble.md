# Lamb Trapped by Collapse

## Operational fields

### Text

If the ceiling collapses on the Lamb, it takes 3d6 damage, digs out in 30 minutes—probably on the north side—and is helpless for 1 round as it exits. Digging can be heard by anyone investigating the rubble.
