# Checks and Saves

## Operational fields

### Text

Nearly all checks are against DC 16: roll a d20, add the relevant stat, and meet or exceed the DC. Saves are an exception and use Dex, Con, or Cha against DC 20. Cha is used for all magical effects and is the most common Save.
