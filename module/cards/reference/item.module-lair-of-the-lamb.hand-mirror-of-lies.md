# Hand Mirror of Lies

## Operational fields

### Text

The crone's coffer in 42 SUNKEN GALLERY contains the Treasure called the Hand Mirror of Lies. It is shaped like the outline of a woman's face, complete with ears and carefully styled hair. The holder chooses what appears in the mirror; it can display anything the holder imagines, including an honest reflection.
