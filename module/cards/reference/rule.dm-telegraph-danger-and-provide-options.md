# Telegraph Danger and Provide Options

## Operational fields

### Text

Horrible threats must leave players either an option to choose another path or options to recover. Presenting a sagging floor and asking whether they enter is an option; an unannounced lethal hazard resolved only by a Dexterity check is mere chance and offers no meaningful choice.
