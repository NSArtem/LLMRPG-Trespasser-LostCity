# Snag Awareness and Repeated Attempts

## Operational fields

### Text

Whether Snag succeeds or fails, the target is aware of the attempt. Out of combat, Snag may be attempted while walking past someone. After the first out-of-combat attempt against the same target, each additional attempt has a 50% chance of being noticed.
