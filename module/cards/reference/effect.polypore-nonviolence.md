# Blue Polypore Aura of Nonviolence

## Operational fields

### Text

While the blue polypores in 33 FUNGUS remain intact, no violence is possible in the room. A character who takes any damage, even a pinprick or self-inflicted damage, gets a Cha Save to resist the effect.
