# Davok Stun and Inertness

## Operational fields

### Text

Davok awakens if touched or if his name is called. If he takes 10 damage in a single round, he is stunned for 1 minute while reconstructing himself. Returning him to the lead-lined chest makes him inert again; the dungeon contains nothing capable of destroying him.
