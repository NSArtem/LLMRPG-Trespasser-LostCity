# Doors

## Operational fields

### Text

At dungeon speed, characters are assumed to listen at doors before opening them; the DM reports audible activity behind a door. A wooden door can be kicked in with a successful Str check in negligible time, but doing so makes Noise; failure takes 10 minutes. Metal doors cannot be kicked in. Locked doors require the appropriate key or lockpicks plus a successful Locksmith roll. Portcullises have fist-sized gaps and are normally locked down so Strength cannot raise them; a nearby ratchet winch often raises them in 1 minute, and they drop in 1 second. A portcullis may instead be locked open. A barred door opens only from the barred side; each bar is a piece of lumber weighing more than 100 pounds, and moving one alone requires a Str check.
