# Makeshift Table-Boat Speed Modifiers

## Operational fields

### Text

A makeshift boat such as a table moves at 10 feet per round. Each paddle adds 1 foot per round up to 15 feet per round, and each person aboard beyond the second reduces its speed by 1 foot per round.
