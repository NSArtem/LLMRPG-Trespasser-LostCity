# Weapons and Armor

## Operational fields

### Text

Fists deal 1d3 damage. Improvised weapons attack at -2 and deal 1d6. Regular weapons deal 1d6. A two-handed weapon wielded by a character with Str 7 or more increases its damage die by one step. A magical weapon increases its damage die by one step. Damage dice increase from 1d6 to 1d8 to 1d10.
