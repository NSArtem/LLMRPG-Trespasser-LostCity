# Stat Blocks

## Operational fields

### Text

Lvl is the same as HD, and an enemy has 1d6 HP per HD. Def is the attack DC required to hit it. Atk gives the attack mode and damage, such as Claws 1d8. Move may be omitted when human-equivalent; escaping a slow pursuer is DC 12 and a fast pursuer is DC 20, and movement may instead name a comparable creature such as Climb spider. Unlisted stats default to 5 unless significantly different. A monster Int check can determine whether it behaves intelligently; failure makes it act like a dumb animal. Dis is disposition. If Dis is absent, the monster is average: it fights while it expects to win, then flees or surrenders.
