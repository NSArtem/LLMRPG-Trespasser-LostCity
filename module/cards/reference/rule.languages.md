# Languages

## Operational fields

### Text

Everyone begins with Common and no other language; nearly everyone, including goblins and morlocks, speaks some form of Common. Foreign languages are usually dead languages and are learned like Skills. A successful Language improvement grants 2 points instead of 1, but reaching 10 still requires a quest. Certain Languages grant an ability at 10 points.
