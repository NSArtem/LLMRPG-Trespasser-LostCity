# Toad Shavings

## Operational fields

### Text

A small bag in 34 CACHE contains 3 doses of toad shavings. When smoked, the user cannot move independently or take actions for 30 minutes, can speak with amphibians and reptiles, and experiences colors, patterns, and croaking noises as indescribably delightful.
