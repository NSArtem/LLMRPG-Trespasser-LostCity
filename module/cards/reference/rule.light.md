# Light

## Operational fields

### Text

The dungeon is pitch black. Torches illuminate 30 feet and go out after two depletions; dropped torches can be recovered. Oil lamps illuminate 30 feet and go out after two depletions; dropping or throwing one creates a 10-foot-wide pool of fire for 3 rounds that deals 1d6 damage per round to anything remaining in contact with it. Candles illuminate 10 feet and last all day. Torches and lanterns deplete only on the regular Encounter Checks made every 30 minutes, not on checks caused by Noise. Light sources are extinguished if they fall in water.
