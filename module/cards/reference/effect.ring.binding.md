# Soul Binding

- ID: `effect.ring.binding`
- Type: `effect`
- Source pages: 26
- References: `effect.ring.unbinding`, `item.ring-of-soul-binding`

## Operational fields

### Text

The ring’s possessor may give it to another person, binding that person’s soul to the giver’s. An unwilling recipient receives a save versus spells to resist.
