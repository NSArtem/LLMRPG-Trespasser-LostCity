# Davok Flesh Absorption

## Operational fields

### Text

While free of the lead-lined chest, Davok attempts once per round to absorb flesh from one person within 30 feet; a Cha Save prevents death. A successful absorption regrows much of Davok’s head and restores a thick, viscous voice.
