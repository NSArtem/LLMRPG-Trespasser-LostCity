# Confusion

## Operational fields

### Text

Range 30 feet; target 1 creature; duration [sum] rounds. Each round the target declares three potential actions that are fully distinct, sharing no objects or verbs. Roll 1d3; the target performs the corresponding action. A save negates the spell. Splittable.
