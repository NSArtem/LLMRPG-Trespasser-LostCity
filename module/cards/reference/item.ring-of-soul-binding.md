## Operational fields

### Text

A fairy magic ring that intertwines two beings’ souls. Its bearer may give it to another person to bind them; an unwilling recipient may save versus spells. The binding ends if the ring is destroyed, dispel magic is cast on the ring or either bound person, either person renounces the bond, or the giver dies. Bound persons can speak through the ring; cross-world separation narrows the boundary between Fairy and the mortal world; on a full moon the wearer can call the other person to them for one night; if the wearer dies, their spirit haunts the ring as a ghost able to manifest only within 10 feet of it.
