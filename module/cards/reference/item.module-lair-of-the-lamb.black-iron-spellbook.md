# Black Iron Spellbook

## Operational fields

### Text

The robed skeleton in 14A SHADRAKUL offers a black iron spellbook. Its covers and pages are polished iron sheets with letters formed by unpolished sections; a chain is woven through the spine to secure it to the owner's waist. Written in Cheoxic, it lets a character begin learning Cheoxic, contains speak with dead and fear, and permits advancement as a Necromancer. Once the reader reaches Necromancer Level 1, Shadrakul asks that character to become her apprentice.
