# Abacus of Vandoh

## Operational fields

### Text

The Abacus of Vandoh is a Treasure in 39 STONE EGG. Made from maple, shale, and lapis lazuli, it performs addition, subtraction, multiplication, and certain square roots. Three times per day it answers a question whose answer is an integer from 2 through 9; secretly roll d100, with 11–100 giving the honest answer, 2–9 giving the rolled number, and 1 or 10 giving no response. It cannot answer about things more than 100 feet away, only understands obdurate physical things, cannot read minds or understand names, struggles to distinguish humans, and cannot understand questions longer than 12 words.
