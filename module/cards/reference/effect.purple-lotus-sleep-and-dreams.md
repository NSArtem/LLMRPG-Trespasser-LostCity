# Purple Lotus Sleep and Dreams

## Operational fields

### Text

Smoking causes sleep for 1d3 hours. On waking, the GM rapidly narrates four dreams in random order: one fun, one about something that happened, one scary, and one about something likely to happen. At least one of the four should be semi-useful.
