# Class Template Selection and Multiclassing

## Operational fields

### Text

Whenever a character gains a template, they choose a class and gain the corresponding template. Lower templates in a class must be gained before higher ones. Multiclassing is unrestricted by selecting Template A from another class. A character may have no more than 4 templates, but beginning at Level 5 may swap templates out. Unless additional classes are unlocked through play, choices are limited to Acrobat, Barbarian, Fighter, and Thief. Wizard, Cleric, Warlock, Knight, Tactician, and races must be unlocked through play.
