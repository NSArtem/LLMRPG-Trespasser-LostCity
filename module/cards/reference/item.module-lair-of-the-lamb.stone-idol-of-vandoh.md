# Stone Idol of Vandoh

## Operational fields

### Text

A stone idol of Vandoh rises from the water in 41 SUNKEN SHRINE. The first person to speak or pray to it has their left hand possessed; the hand holds up a number of fingers equal to the ghouls remaining in the dungeon and clenches when a ghoul is encountered.
