### Text

Death does not end the ring's bond. Sir Chyde's spirit is called back to linger as a phantom in his tomb; the princess's prison in Fairy and the tomb in the mortal world are slowly drawn together until the two realms nearly touch; and the princess and knight can communicate verbally while dreaming of reunion and marriage.
