# Treasure and Milestone Level Ups

## Operational fields

### Text

The dungeon presents 8 advancement objectives: three Milestones and five Treasures. The listed objectives are sitting on the throne in 7 THRONE; finding the Ruby Ring of Wisdom in 8 PIT; finding the Jewelled Crab Bracer in 19 PRIESTS; removing the bar from 21 HEAVY DOOR or killing the Lamb; finding the Crysmere Blade of Charcorra in 27 BALLISTA; finding the Abacus of Vandoh in 39 STONE EGG; finding the Hand Mirror of Lies in 42 SUNKEN GALLERY; and escaping through 46B TOWER.
