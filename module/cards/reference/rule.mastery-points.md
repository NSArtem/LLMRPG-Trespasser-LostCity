# Mastery Points

## Operational fields

### Text

A character gains the first Mastery Point at 5th level and one at every later level. A Mastery Point may alter an adjacent ally’s in-game d20 roll or damage roll after the dice land. It cannot affect magic dice, skill-up attempts, stat-up attempts, natural 1s or 20s, or the user’s own roll. It may turn a near miss into a success, reduce damage an ally takes, or increase damage an ally deals. Mastery Points replenish after a full night’s rest.
