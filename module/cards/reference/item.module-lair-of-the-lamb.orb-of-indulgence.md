# Orb of Indulgence

## Operational fields

### Text

The armored corpse beyond the portcullis carries an Orb of Indulgence worth 500s. It is a silver orb about 1 inch across that immediately forgives the bearer's next significant sin, after which it corrodes. Orbs are sold but tightly controlled; the source says finding one makes resale to the Church the only ethical option. Rumors claim golden orbs can forgive even mortal sins.
