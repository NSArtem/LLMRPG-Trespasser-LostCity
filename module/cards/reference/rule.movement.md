# Movement

## Operational fields

### Text

Difficult movement such as climbing, jumping, and fleeing uses a Move check, usually DC 16. A level 0 Peasant has Move 5. Falling deals 1d6 damage per 10 feet fallen. A character who intentionally jumps down can reduce the damage by 1d6 with a successful Dex check.
