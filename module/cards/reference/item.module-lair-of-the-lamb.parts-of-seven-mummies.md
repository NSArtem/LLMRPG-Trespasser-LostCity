# Parts of Seven Mummies

## Operational fields

### Text

The crypts hold 7 mummified left arms in 25A, 7 right arms in 25B, 2 heads in 25C, 7 left legs in 25D, 7 torsos in 25E, and 7 right legs in 25F. Five additional costumed heads are in 29 STAGE. These parts can be reassembled and returned for the Vandoh favor outcome.
