# Shendormu’s Favor Progression

## Operational fields

### Text

Favor from Shendormu grants exactly one use of control emotions and permanently turns the recipient’s eyes green. On the first morning after the recipient leaves the dungeon, a private green glow leads to the Many-Layered Hall, where the recipient is offered an optional priesthood and access to level as a cleric of Shendormu.
