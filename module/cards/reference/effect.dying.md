# Dying

## Operational fields

### Text

When a non-level-0 character takes damage beyond the amount remaining in HP, the excess becomes Lethal Damage; gaining any Lethal Damage makes the character fall unconscious and begin Dying. At 10 Lethal Damage, the character dies.
