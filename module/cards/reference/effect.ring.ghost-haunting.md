# Ghost Bound to the Ring

- ID: `effect.ring.ghost-haunting`
- Type: `effect`
- Source pages: 26
- References: `item.ring-of-soul-binding`, `situation.epilogue.ring-stolen`

## Operational fields

### Text

If the ring’s wearer dies, their spirit cannot rest and haunts the ring as a ghost. The ghost can manifest only while within 10 feet of the ring.
