# Pursuit

## Operational fields

### Text

First choose the direction of flight. The slowest party member makes a Movement check, usually DC 16; the Lamb instead has Slow DC 12. The party may drop items or alter the environment to slow pursuit. On failure, the fight moves into the next room and the pursuer takes a standard action, usually an attack. On success, the party enters the next room and the pursuer arrives at the end of the round. If the pursuer does not know the route, it chooses a random direction and continues for 1d4 rooms, preferring rooms it has not searched. Running past an enemy lets it make all its normal attacks. Hungry or Greedy enemies may stop for safely accessible food or money unless they have another reason to continue.
