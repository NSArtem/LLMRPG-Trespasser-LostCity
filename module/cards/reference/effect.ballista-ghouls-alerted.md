# Gerdith Alerted by Noise or Light

## Operational fields

### Text

Combat, a portcullis winch, a dropped sarcophagus lid, or torchlight in the rooms Gerdith can see alerts her. Light entering 25E, 25F, 25G, or 29 STAGE is noticed by the ghouls in 27 BALLISTA.
