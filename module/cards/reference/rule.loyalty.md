# Loyalty

## Operational fields

### Text

Each hireling begins with Loyalty 5. Asking for a dangerous task outside the hireling’s job description requires a Loyalty check and always reduces Loyalty by 1, whether accepted or not. Good treatment such as extra pay, extra safety, or respect raises Loyalty by 1 or 2. Mistreatment lowers it by 1, 1d4, or 1d6. At Loyalty 0, the hireling leaves at the earliest opportunity.
