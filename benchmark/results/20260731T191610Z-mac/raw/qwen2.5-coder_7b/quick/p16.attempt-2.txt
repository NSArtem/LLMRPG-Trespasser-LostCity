red-temple,role,public,The Red Temple worships Shendormu, the God of Fungus, Mindlessness, and Community.
temple-of-true-power,role,public,The Temple of True Power is known for its focus on strength and size.
lamb,visible,discoverable,One of the secrets that the White Temple keeps is called the Lamb. It was born of a sheep and is a holy child of Vandoh.
